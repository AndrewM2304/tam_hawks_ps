## Density and flow gates

- For each arena draw **two genuine returnable lines** and one safe bypass on the actual final layout. A line names its launch, capture/manual/air decisions, landing and usable next choice; it cannot rely on teleports, forced speed or instantaneous turns. Duplicating a clockwise/counterclockwise perimeter is insufficient.
- At an ordinary 8–10 m/s approach, use **about 3 seconds without a meaningful skating/route choice** as a review trigger. This is a design target, not a hard timer or claim that every 3-second rest is bad. Necessary run-ups/recovery and deliberate reveals need explicit justification. Measure actual controls instead of inferring elapsed time from distance alone.
- A landing should reveal the next local option without a blind90° chase-camera turn; approach/readability and input margin matter more than an inflated rail count. Bound scenery as scenery through architecture, gradient, planting or a clearly visible barrier.
- Recompute the [spatial inventory](../testing/level-density/README.md), but never optimize the 8/15 m proxy by adding decorative curbs or deleting useful flat. Judge novice discovery, route choice, vertical overlap, repeatable returns and performance together.
