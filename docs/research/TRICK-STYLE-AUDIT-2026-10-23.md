## Reference and uncertainty

Primary reference: Activision's original THPS3 PS2 instruction manual, [archived scan](https://impzone.club/pdf/thps3.pdf) and [readable transcription](https://manualzz.com/doc/7113809/-user-manual-). It distinguishes ground ollie height from quarter-pipe air, charge/release, directional tricks, orientation-dependent grinds, wallrides, landing reverts and combo links. It also documents nollies, no-comply/boneless variants, held grabs, hidden combinations, stance bonuses, in-combo SPECIAL availability, repeat devaluation and quicker bail recovery. These are feature evidence, not calibrated physical constants or skeletal animation timings. Scan/transcription glyphs differ; do not transplant corrupted button symbols. Main PS2 controls/stat sections support the family comparison; N64-port and remake features are not silently treated as identical.

Pinned THPS1 reconstruction `fa128f5ba222e0f24a9bff019d59262b79141e48`, [HandleJump](https://github.com/LANCommander/THPS/blob/fa128f5ba222e0f24a9bff019d59262b79141e48/decomp/src/physics/handlejump.cpp), has charge/stat/support-dependent jump paths and animation calls. It does not expose the requested front/rear-foot timing as a verified THPS3 skeletal curve. Our staged analytic motion follows the owner's requirement. No source-game code, clips, models or manual imagery is bundled. Read-only source/manual hashes are in [the source receipt](TRICK-STYLE-SOURCES-2026-10-23.json); cached research stays ignored.

A straight-on foot-powered rebound is an explicitly requested design addition. The inspected original PS2 manual does not establish its exact activation, allowable angle or mechanics; do not present later-series wallplants, wallies from a wallride, and lip handplants as one verified THPS3 move. Distinguish them in implementation and tests.

## Grind angles, styles and transitions

[GrindRide](../../game/tricks/grind_ride.gd) already supports signed travel on straight, sloped and curved authored rails/edges/coping. Entry uses horizontal velocity versus line tangent with `abs(dot)>=cos(65°)`, bounded radius/vertical reach, limited upward velocity and actual clearance. Eligible oblique approaches from either travel direction therefore exist; near-perpendicular travel is rejected. Swept capture handles a missed window without widening the envelope. A native yard approach with the new shorter flight exposed a1.08467m endpoint correction in one tick: the existing radius plus completed step can exceed1m. The closer-request fixture retains its<1m continuity gate; a general correction-distance/visual-contact refinement remains a grind-style task, not a globally fixed behavior. The player then aligns root facing to the tangent and clears visual air spin.

The approach angle is not the grind style. Board orientation relative to the line is not retained/selected for scoring, `DirectionalTricks` has no grind category, and all catches share `rail_grind`. There is no distinct 50-50, nosegrind,5-0, boardslide/lipslide, noseslide/tailslide, smith/feeble, crooked/overcrook or blunt/noseblunt contact/scoring state. A visually parallel generic ride resembles a basic grind, but is not evidence that the full style set works. No in-grind style-switch, shuffle, revert-to-other-grind, grind flip-out/recatch style identity or truck/deck-specific balance axis is implemented. Existing jump-off and actual recapture are foundations, not those variants.

Next: keep safe travel/clearance unchanged while defining style from fresh directional input plus pre-capture board-to-line heading/contact side. Preserve physical travel tangent independently from local board orientation. Broad entry tests need both directions, oblique travel and perpendicular board orientation on parallel travel, frontside/backside, regular/fakie, slopes/bends, missed-end capture, obstacles and stale held input. Do not admit65°+captures by merely increasing a radius, relabel every ride50-50, or use cosmetic yaw as proof of valid contacts.

## Physics and animation findings for the immediate fix

The rejected flat profile shared tap8/full13 m/s with ramp pop. At gravity34, ideal full-charge ballistic rise is2.485m; measured body rise~2.605m. This is not a verified THPS3 value and it overcompensates for the ramp request. The new candidate separates flat7.2/full9from ramp8/full13, preserves gravity/momentum and uses an original short nose-up→level-off sequence. Front sole must visibly lead rear sole, then both meet the level board; falling off a ledge must not invent an Ollie. Actual measurements, natural short lines, limb contacts, quiet camera, strong ramp tests, native timing/captures and the required full suite must establish the new source. No human approval is inferred from a passing test.

Existing landing behavior is forgiving rather than a complete self-righting system: `_finish_landing` emits a clean landing after native eligible support regardless of visual spin heading. The upright physical capsule means the visual body is not a free rolling board-body rigid simulation. The requested assistance needs explicit presentation/eligibility design, not just looser collision or silently scoring a revert. The complete audit findings are actionable gaps, not claims that all missing mechanics were implemented in this slice.
