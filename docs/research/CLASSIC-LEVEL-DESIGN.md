# Classic skating level reference notes

Reviewed **17 October 2026** for the owner's request to combine the qualities of San Francisco, London, Los Angeles and the School levels in an original Glasgow skating map. This is a senior skate-game design review perspective, not a claim of employment by Neversoft, Activision or another developer. The review uses written walkthrough evidence and Tam Hawks source/rendered captures; no commercial game was launched or its original map directly inspected during this session.

## Which games are being compared

For the original console versions covered by the inspected guides, **San Francisco and London are THPS4**, **Los Angeles is THPS3**, **School is THPS1**, and **School II/School 2 is THPS2**. These notes do not treat the 2025 THPS3+4 remake as the original layout/mechanics reference. The owner's intended combination remains clear: several memorable skating spaces, angled streets and corridors, useful height, routes that cross and reconnect, and local character.

## Original Glasgow design translation

| Reference quality inferred from those routes | Original Glasgow application | Evidence needed here |
|---|---|---|
| A local landmark organizes movement; it also has several approach/release directions. | Exchange fountain coping, civic steps, station canopy/platform, market loading court and Clyde bridge become the anchors. Add connecting ledges/banks around those anchors rather than isolated feature rows. | Real capture/travel/release and a visible next landing at production chase height. A landmark mesh alone does not establish flow. |
| Tight connections and broad spaces alternate, so the player recognizes separate areas. | A bent Merchant Close through building islands connects the station street to the civic back lane. The wider pocket near its corner gives turning and landing room. | Solid enclosure, two usable exits, no camera pinching or collision snags; approach captures from both mouths. |
| Higher routes and lower routes overlap, producing choices within a compact footprint. | Suspended station decks leave a playable undercroft on the concourse below, while retaining the existing platform gap above. | Lower capsule/head clearance, no filled collision beneath the upper deck, and supported access to both routes. |
| A route changes direction/height and then reconnects; a failed advanced attempt can return to play. | Park cutback rejoins the station approach; Clyde landing enters an angled quay pocket and rejoins the promenade. Continuous bridge and path options remain accessible. | Bidirectional ground traversal, supported seams, clear recovery and no required unsupported maneuver. |
| Goals reveal relationships already useful for free skating. | Existing HAWKS/market/recording goals teach the city; local fictional challenge-givers can draw attention to undercroft, fountain, bridge and park return without expanding saved career requirements by accident. | Challenge events must come from actual player movement and resolve once. Merely standing near a feature must not fake a trick or gap. |
| A place contains a compact chain of actions, then space to decide the next route. | Aim for low ledge → bank/air → manual or second grind links near each pocket; reserve broad straight segments for approaches/landings and visual reveals. | A real linked combo with production timing, alongside a novice supported bypass. Distance alone does not prove combo feasibility. |

These principles do not require copying a level outline, its landmark ordering or its challenge scripts. A recognisable city can have broadly orthogonal streets; the playable route graph, intersecting heights, enclosures and diagonal choices must supply variety. Changing the outside silhouette while leaving the same empty rectangular skating courts would not satisfy the request.

## Mechanics boundary

Implemented foundations include banks, stairs as optional drops, ollies, flip/grab/spin, manuals, rail/ledge/curved coping grinds, reverts, named airborne gaps, local recovery and two specials. The controller classifies floors up to 56°. **Near-vertical quarter-pipes/bowls, vert/lip tricks, wallrides/wallies, spine transfers, skitching, vehicle interactions and a dynamic world-unlock system are not currently enabled.** A curved low bank or a decorative bowl is not proof of vert skating. Keep any route needing those features optional and explicitly labelled future work until mechanics and camera tests exist.

Next reference work, if needed, is a dated human inspection of authorized original-game play/footage or a credible developer interview, recording exact version and timestamps. That can refine pacing and sightlines; it must not be retroactively described as evidence obtained in this session. Human enjoyment, difficulty and authentic Glasgow personality remain playtest/owner judgement.
