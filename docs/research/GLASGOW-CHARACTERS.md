## Access evidence and limits

- DuckDuckGo's free HTML search returned relevant links, after which the actual Glasgow Live, VICE and City Live articles returned HTTP 200 and their text was inspected.
- Bing returned HTTP 200 but several results did not match the submitted quoted queries. Those results were rejected as evidence. A successful HTTP response alone does not verify a claim.
- An attempted council busking URL returned HTTP 403. A photography page about St Enoch returned HTTP 503. Neither establishes facts here. Initial guessed Glasgow Live category URLs returned 404; actual article URLs above subsequently worked.
- This does not change the earlier Google Maps / Street View access limitation or provide a live connection to the user's local Chrome.

## Character rules derived for this game

Make the local character a participant with a point of view: a busker wants a strong finale; a market trader wants an absurd delivery kept intact; a park regular sets an improbably precise line. Use warm understatement and a small amount of understandable local phrasing. Caption every instruction and keep the objective readable without understanding dialect.

Use **fictional people**, original clothing and dialogue. “Mo” is an original musician, not a renamed depiction of one of the sources. The cleared generic MakeHuman anatomical derivative supplies a human head/hands; original garments and instrument supply the rest. There is no claim of a Glasgow accent: the encounter has captions and animation, with no new voice recording. Its quiet spatial instrument is original local PCM synthesis of an open chord, with no imported song or source performance.

The owner's river-prank suggestion is captured as a design direction for harmless, fictional slapstick. Make floating foam props, an inflatable mascot or an explicitly willing festival stunt crew the target. Avoid making a real local performer or a caricature of poverty/addiction the butt of a violent challenge. This preserves the cheeky physical gag and gives it a visible float/rescue payoff.
