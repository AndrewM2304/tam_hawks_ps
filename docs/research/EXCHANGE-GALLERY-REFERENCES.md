## What the gallery evidence supports

E1 describes **cream and yellow ashlar**, an imposing Corinthian pedimented front with **eight columns**, and the giant order standing on a **low plinth**. It also describes a coffered portico ceiling, five tripartite doorways with panelled pilasters, ordered upper windows and decorative cast-iron railings/pedestals around the basement. Those facts support a continuous, architecturally joined base and a recessed entrance; they do not supply a numerical elevation for the game's landing.

E2/E3 visibly show fluted shafts, carved capitals and stacked round mouldings at the column feet. A short broad flight of shallow stone steps runs across the entrance. The dark doorway plane sits behind the front columns, with the portico overhead. Horizontal foundation/step courses meet the column bases rather than covering their mouldings. In E2/E4 people sit on the low step/end-block edges, giving a useful sense of function without providing measured heights.

E4 is the clearest ground-detail reference: wide rectangular grey/dark paving slabs meet lighter stone step and coping bands. The low end return beside the column bases has a rounded/canted profile. A broad open paved apron separates the entrance from the street; narrow pale bands and changes in paving level help distinguish its edges. The photograph does not establish the stone species of every slab, exact step count across the whole frontage, accessible-entry dimensions or the current street configuration.

Colours should therefore read as restrained warm-grey/cream architectural stone, darker weathered recesses and legible lighter step/cap edges. Actual stone repair is documented by E6, but the public text does not justify inventing cracks, scaffolding or current conservation work as permanent scenery.

## West boundary and seating cues

E5 looks toward the **western bank building**, not the gallery's eastern portico. Its six-column Ionic frontage and broad bank steps must not be mistaken for GoMA's eight-column Corinthian front. The useful evidence for this bounded slice is at ground level: gently curved low pale coping/step bands, broad paving, dark decorative basement railings and a small outdoor café enclosure with umbrella/planters at the outer edge. An open passage remains visible through the western arch.

These observations support an edge where seating or low stonework belongs to the paving/facade composition while leaving a clear public route. They do not verify a specific present-day café, a permanent bench inventory or a 0.45 m raised shopfront platform. No current tenant names or shop order should be derived from these older images. The game's west shopfront remains a fictional composite.

## Applying the evidence without changing the bounded scope

- **Keep the column feet and threshold coherent.** The planned gallery landing at Y2.65 is 0.65 m above the existing authored Y2 gallery floor. Its foundation, column-base mouldings and recessed threshold should meet that landing deliberately. Simply covering a base originally placed at Y2 would hide the observed footing hierarchy. A local footing/threshold adjustment is enough; this pass does not call for rebuilding the whole gallery or statue.
- **Distinguish baseline and new composition.** The baseline four-column game portico simplified the documented eight-column front. The bounded new pass is authorized to use an original eight-column entrance, integrating bases and threshold at the authored Y2.65 landing while retaining the existing roof datum. The new chamfered terrace, side banks and skateable low ledge remain authored additions, even when their stone courses and edge shapes use the inspected references.
- **Use the paving/edge relationship.** Distinguish paved riding surface, vertical retaining face and a modest cap/step band. Close the foundation into the facade so it neither floats nor leaves an open underside. Decorative colour or a thin trim alone is not a supported landing surface.
- **Keep west seating at the margin.** The proposed Y0.45 west corner within X−35…−27/Z−2…10 and bank toward X−24 are gameplay dimensions, not measurements from E5. Preserve the agreed passage, planter and station approach. Any angled/curved seating ledge is an original interpretation; do not claim an exact real bench.

The proposed gallery footprint X−10…10/Z−42…−31, its 0.65 m rise, the west 0.45 m rise and the game's fountain/stair arrangement are all explicitly authored. The bounded implementation keeps the protected routes, current statue, canonical goals and accepted collision contracts; these observations do not call for a larger historical reconstruction. The geometry owner must still verify support/collision joins and captured views at the real chase camera.
