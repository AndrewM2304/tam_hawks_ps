## Assessment of the previous changes

The last turn corrected an actual missing behavior: hills now exchange uphill/downhill motion through signed board-forward velocity. It preserved collision-adjusted airborne travel, shortened excessive release coasting, separated ground carving from air correction and removed camera sensitivity from physical steering. The prior real 20° bank rollback and 1,264 passing checks establish those bounded changes. They do not establish classic skate-game control semantics, a forgiving human capture envelope or good handling of every new negative-speed state.

The remaining mismatch is broader than friction. Our current ollie pops on the press, steering produces a particularly wide high-speed arc, A/D or the left stick bends flight without visibly spinning the rider, and landing always succeeds irrespective unfinished tricks or the rendered board angle. A single 180 spin does not install a fakie landing stance; spins are cosmetic and reset toward the old travel heading. These choices were deliberately forgiving in the early prototype. They should now be explicit design choices with player feedback, rather than interpreted as completed Tony Hawk-like handling.

## Concrete comparisons

**Ground turning.** In [our response helper](../../game/player/skate_response.gd), steady yaw rate falls from 2.3 rad/s at rest to 1.1 rad/s at 15 m/s, with a 0.10 s response time. With negligible slip, the geometric radius is speed divided by yaw rate. NPS [ground steering](https://github.com/TinyBitAdventures/not-pro-skater/blob/d18803ae9f2daecf7f3ae276b2f14c1eb0ed5988/game/scripts/skater/skater.gd#L640-L667) uses [default rates](https://github.com/TinyBitAdventures/not-pro-skater/blob/d18803ae9f2daecf7f3ae276b2f14c1eb0ed5988/game/scripts/skater/skate_tuning.gd#L42-L47) of 6.5→3.3 rad/s over 0→12 m/s. Its default push cap is 9 m/s. These are source-derived steady-state circles, not measured trajectories or commercial-game constants:

| Speed | Our yaw / radius | NPS default yaw / radius |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5 m/s | 1.90 rad/s / 2.63 m | 5.17 rad/s / 0.97 m |
| 9 m/s | 1.58 rad/s / 5.70 m | 4.10 rad/s / 2.20 m |
| 12 m/s | 1.34 rad/s / 8.96 m | 3.30 rad/s / 3.64 m |
| 15 m/s | 1.10 rad/s / 13.64 m | 3.30 rad/s / 4.55 m |

Our full-speed circle therefore needs roughly 27.3 m diameter, with an additional initial response delay. The prior actual-input probe measured only 56.72° in the first full-speed second. This can feel unresponsive in narrow streets even though the yaw interpolation is smooth. Do not blindly triple the rate: compare bounded 1.6/2.0/2.4 rad/s full-speed candidates on the existing fountain, market and close turns, with low-speed steering retained. A held brake/kickturn can provide a deliberate faster reversal without making straight analogue travel twitchy. Keep camera sensitivity separate.

**Coast versus slopes.** Our level-ground law reaches 5.976 m/s after three seconds from 15 and stops after about 6.93 s; the actual city can lose more to contact. NPS default coast uses exponential 0.05/s resistance and would retain 12.911 m/s after the same three seconds on an ideal flat plane. Copying that constant would restore the owner's previous excessive-coasting complaint. Reuse the decomposition into forward/lateral velocity, not its tune wholesale. Ground tangent gravity must remain separate from drag/braking; a descending hill should accelerate while grounded brakes can hold a stopped board.

**Pop timing.** Our 8.8 m/s fixed impulse on `just_pressed` yields the measured 0.833 s flat ollie. NPS [input edge/buffer handling](https://github.com/TinyBitAdventures/not-pro-skater/blob/d18803ae9f2daecf7f3ae276b2f14c1eb0ed5988/game/scripts/skater/skater.gd#L573-L594) and [ground pop](https://github.com/TinyBitAdventures/not-pro-skater/blob/d18803ae9f2daecf7f3ae276b2f14c1eb0ed5988/game/scripts/skater/skater.gd#L723-L745) support crouch while held, one pop on release, and late release shortly past the lip. Its default charge span is 0.45 s, impulse 7.8→10.0 m/s, coyote 0.11 s and request buffer 0.14 s. GodotSkate [input handling](https://github.com/3deric/godot-skateboarding-game/blob/4fad39ecfb21e79ca4799b90e77ef6c1edfa7928/Skategame/Scripts/Player/character_input.gd) also buffers Jump release and uses held Jump for ground acceleration. We already have 0.10 s coyote and 0.12 s press buffering. Hold/release with a visible crouch is the next larger handling change, with an explicit tap-style fallback if wanted. It needs revised input-driven routes and reset/pause handling; changing only the input edge would make short taps fail between ticks.

**Flight direction and spin.** Ours rotates travel by at most 0.50 rad/s using A/D/left stick and rotates the drawn rider at 7.2 rad/s using Q/E/shoulders. Over a nominal 0.8 s jump that is at most ~23° travel correction versus ~330° visible spin; the two have different buttons. NPS [_air](https://github.com/TinyBitAdventures/not-pro-skater/blob/d18803ae9f2daecf7f3ae276b2f14c1eb0ed5988/game/scripts/skater/skater.gd#L1098-L1143) uses the lateral stick for visible yaw and separate bounded world-direction acceleration for air correction. GodotSkate [air state](https://github.com/3deric/godot-skateboarding-game/blob/4fad39ecfb21e79ca4799b90e77ef6c1edfa7928/Skategame/Scripts/Player/Player_States/player_air.gd#L24-L36) rotates the rider transform without reconstructing travel. Prefer a reviewed control mode where left/right produces the expected visible spin, with shoulders as useful alternatives; retain the takeoff regression and avoid reinstating the old curved-flight boost.

**Landings and linking.** NPS [_land](https://github.com/TinyBitAdventures/not-pro-skater/blob/d18803ae9f2daecf7f3ae276b2f14c1eb0ed5988/game/scripts/skater/skater.gd#L1304-L1387) compares heading against actual travel, treats 180° as fakie, grades a moderate error as sketchy, and aligns a near landing. It stores an early manual request before contact. Its default clean/bail thresholds are 35°/58°, manual request 0.45 s and revert window 0.35 s; those are reference choices. Our unconditional clean landing deliberately avoids punishment but removes that feedback and stance meaning. First add early input requests and readable regular/fakie/assisted landing state. Evaluate speed loss for visibly sideways or unfinished tricks before enabling new bails; adding strict failure globally would make the present controls harder without fixing their affordances. Keep the existing authoritative 0.35 s combo bank deadline independent of request buffers.

## Signed-speed review at the previous checkpoint

These are findings against committed `315d245`, so later integration may resolve them. Baseline hashes: `skater.gd` `f0c80f26cc4bb5becd2baf2c5ee5457d8ca2e10f563e74d56481794e34648627`; `skate_response.gd` `cac4608804b15c88228ba1889e7ce3b7654c6b0c6f101420c1786c745d22dfc3`.

| Finding | Classification / consequence | Bounded remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Gravity still runs after `set_controls_enabled(false)` resets velocity. Subsequent grounded updates coast and apply slope gravity. | State-control defect: an input-disabled rider can roll away on a bank. Scalar recurrence on a 20° plane predicts −1.020 m/s after one second, matching the previous ordinary rollback behavior. This is a calculation, not a new physics run. | Explicitly retain the disabled horizontal stop and prove it on real supported uphill/downhill bodies, including title/results input boundaries. |
| Brake moves signed speed toward zero **before** every frame's gravity. | Brake-hold defect: at rest on a 20° bank, each 60 Hz tick reintroduces 0.03511 m/s downhill, approximately 3.5 cm/s creep. | Integrate slope first or provide grounded static hold when mapped braking reaches zero. Do not suppress unbraked rollback. |
| Manual eligibility and exit still use positive `speed >= 2` / `speed < 2`. | Newly exposed design gap: a rider rolling backwards at 5 m/s cannot manual and an existing manual exits at sign change. Normal world speed is now signed, while this feature originally assumed an unsigned magnitude. | Decide whether fakie/rollback manuals are supported, then use magnitude plus explicit stance/balance semantics. Do not replace every signed-speed use indiscriminately. |
| Ground speed projects actual travel onto board forward; air speed becomes horizontal magnitude, while velocity keeps its real sign. | Momentum is preserved across a backwards ollie, but the public field changes meaning across states. Consumers cannot infer travel direction from `speed` while airborne. | Document signed ground speed versus magnitude air speed; use real velocity/dot products for direction-sensitive camera and stance logic. Test rollback→ollie→landing without forward velocity reconstruction. |
| Camera rear is always `rider.global_basis.z`; lookahead points along body forward. | Framing risk: while rolling or jumping backwards the camera remains in the direction of travel and looks uphill. This is source-confirmed selection behavior, not a newly observed occlusion or first-person failure. | Retain a stable facing near zero; after sustained reverse motion, evaluate a travel-facing chase with hysteresis rather than an abrupt 180° cut. Capture the actual motion before deciding. |

Steering now uses `abs(speed)` for rate, which prevents nonsensical negative interpolation. Whether reverse steering should rotate body-relative or travel-relative is a control choice, not an automatic bug; test both with a short bank rollback and explicit controller wording. HUD/audio already use magnitudes in their reviewed paths. Do not undo correct negative tangent momentum merely to make all values positive.

**Integration follow-up:** root's subsequent draft now applies slope gravity before braking, suppresses it while controls are disabled, and uses speed magnitude for manual admission/exit. It adds consumable 0.12 s flip/grab/manual requests, clears them on disable/overtime/reset/capture, and checks the completed actual movement for a skipped rail window with current-origin collision clearance and `get_real_velocity()`. A read-only source review found no duplicate award or new overtime bypass in those changes. New controller defaults are Triangle grind, R2 revert and R3 restart, with no duplicate default event tokens; Revert is now permitted in the saved-axis validator. A saved override explicitly using the old Triangle restart may conflict with the new untouched grind default: existing atomic rejection preserves play and reports the control conflict, and Reset controls restores the new defaults. A player migration policy can improve that recovery later. Root/QE owns focused and complete test receipts in STATUS; this source review does not assert the final integrated result. The hold/release, turning, stance and reverse-camera recommendations remain open.

## Human-error and physical-input matrix

The existing route tests use ordinary action events, but many choose exact timing and route-center approaches. They are useful collision/score regressions and cannot estimate a person's error distribution. Add deterministic perturbation trials with declared misses and successes, then use the same routes for a short real Mac playtest. Suggested values below are initial coverage, not claimed acceptance thresholds or commercial-game tuning.

| Trial | Perturbations to record | Useful observation |
|---|---|---|
| Rail approach/catch | Speeds 4/8/12/15 m/s; ±8°/15°/25° approach; lateral ±0.15/0.30/0.50/0.75 m; request −0.18/−0.12/−0.08/−0.04/0/+0.04/+0.08/+0.12 s around the nominal catch. Start with small one-variable sweeps, then seeded combinations. | Capture percentage by offset/timing, maximum snap correction, clear release momentum, understandable failure. A sweep must not let a nearby behind-wall rail win. Include reverse, bends, descending/ascending and nearby competing rails. |
| Analogue steering | Real joypad events at magnitudes 0.10/0.18/0.22/0.35/0.60/1.0, slow crossings and noisy values around the configured 0.20 deadzone. Both left/right; short and held presses. | No idle drift, usable small turns, stable mapping after remap. `Input.action_press` alone bypasses the physical axis path and cannot certify this. |
| Pop/release | Tap shorter than a physics tick; 0.10/0.25/0.45 s hold; release −0.12/−0.06/0/+0.06/+0.12 s around a lip; press while falling; pause/reset/disable between hold and release. | Exactly one pop, predictable height, no unexpected auto-pop after reset, clear coyote/buffer boundary. |
| Air trick/link | Flip/grab 0.12/0.06 s before takeoff, after takeoff and near contact; early manual/revert at −0.15/−0.08/0/+0.08/+0.15 s around contact. | Request consumed once in eligible state; no stale score/reentry or bank-deadline extension; visible result consistent with accepted input. |
| Signed travel | Supported bank rest with no input, brake and disabled controls; uphill stop→rollback→ollie→land; reverse turn and manual attempts. | Hold where requested, roll otherwise; preserved backwards momentum; readable camera/stance; no forward jump when the sign changes. |
| Human route | Existing fountain circle, Barras close→rail→market, Exchange uphill/downhill and a Clyde approach. Keyboard then wired/Bluetooth DualSense on declared Mac. | Three repeat attempts at each line, unintended bails/catches, exact missed input, turn overshoot, camera comfort and frame-time spikes. Report novice/experienced feedback separately instead of declaring “fun” from automated completion. |

Do not solve the margin by expanding every capture radius or making every landing succeed. Forgiveness should accept a plausible near miss, consume the intended action and give stable feedback while refusing blocked or unrelated geometry. Root/QE records the actual new results; this research runs no full suite or graphical job.
