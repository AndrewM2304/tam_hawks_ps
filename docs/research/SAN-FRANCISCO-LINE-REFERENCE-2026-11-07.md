# San Francisco reference and the level replan

Owner direction,7 November2026: the present city still has too much empty travel;
cut or shrink areas, study existing examples and replan. The subsequent correction
is explicit: **Warehouse is too small; think San Francisco.** The112×88m Merchant
Yard draft is superseded, not approved or implemented. Preserve its historical
review and unresolved portal/headroom findings as evidence about that draft.

Use the San Francisco games as references for organisation and sustained skating
at neighbourhood scale. Keep an original Glasgow setting and original geometry;
the owner has not requested a franchise replica or a change of city identity.

The owner's subsequent direction adds **Los Angeles and London**, requests
overall and section sketches instead of reliance on existing geometry, and
explicitly retains Glasgow identity. The [original concept boards](../levels/MERCHANT-DISTRICT-CONCEPTS-2026-11-07/README.md)
are now the visual starting point. The existing40×30m facility and long Kelvingrove
climb are not design requirements for the replacement.

## Los Angeles and London additions

The [original THPS3 Los Angeles guide](https://www.tonyhawkguide.com/pro-skater-3/los-angeles.html)
describes a quarter-to-roof transfer, connected elevated buildings and rails on
both sides of an air gap. The transferable lesson is shared arrivals between
street and roof lines. Our depot/roof cut should return to useful ground skating
rather than terminate at a collectible or require a long trip back. This is a
guide-based spatial inference, not inspected LA gameplay footage or a copied route.

The [THPS4 London guide for PS2/GC/Xbox](https://planettonyhawk.gamespy.com/Viewd033.html?id=6&view=thps4_faqs_guides.Detail)
explicitly links ledges, manuals, a quarter pipe, an upper ledge and wires. Its
Southbank area uses varied objects in one pocket. Apply that relationship to
our market/arcade: short useful flats should join different surface types, with
several next choices and a returned upper route.

Root also inspected twelve five-second samples from
[GameSpot's THPS4 Gameplay Movie4](https://www.gamespot.com/videos/tony-hawks-pro-skater-4-gameplay-movie-4/2300-6073942/),
whose publisher description identifies London. The native640×360/60.066667s clip
shows street-edge transitions and a terraced fountain/civic space with adjoining
ledges and steps. It contains edited attempts/goal interactions, not one continuous
combo or a complete map tour. The4,304,768-byte public clip and sample sheet stay
ignored locally. Do not conflate original console layouts with PS1/GBA ports or
the2025 remake while applying these observations.

## Diagnosis of our current layout

The [complete gap/line inventory](GAP-LINE-CONNECTIVITY-2026-11-07.md) describes
eight scored gaps,66 world routes/67 HUD routes and242 unique grind guides. That
quantity has not produced sufficient density. A156m quay straight and103m
promenade dominate travel, while many side areas share few useful entries or
returns. A demonstrated east-quay connection still banks twice; two good separate
runs are not one closed combo circuit. Manual coasting cannot repair the spatial
problem: from6m/s, the existing0.75m/s² drag leaves only about21.33m before speed
falls to2m/s, even before turns or imperfect execution.

The problem is the placement and relationship of spaces. Adding a rail beside a
long empty road leaves the road empty after that rail. Decorations improve the
image without providing a next trick. Enlarging a small skatepark produces the
same issue if its useful features move farther apart.

## Direction for the replacement

Build a medium urban neighbourhood from several closely adjoining skate spaces,
separated by believable building blocks and changes in height. Streets themselves
must support lines: pavement ledges, descending handrails, banks at street ends,
wallride exits, accessible terraces and visible landings. Roof routes should
cross ground routes and return to them; useful underpasses should reuse the space
beneath elevated lines. A central court can serve as an interchange rather than
an empty flat square between distant attractions.

Retain breathing room where the player needs to see, land or turn. Density is
measured by useful choices and achievable next contacts, not clutter or collision
objects per square metre. Keep clear beginner bypasses and believable full-size
skaters, boards, stairs, landings and buildings. Do not uniformly shrink actors,
trick distances or the existing city as a shortcut.

The revised plan must name actual starts, approach speeds, support elevations,
landing widths, next contacts and returns. Every portal must remove the relevant
solid walls, support decks and grind guides; arrows through a building are not a
connection. Upper routes need genuine trick headroom. Street ends need a useful
return or a deliberate boundary; hidden teleports do not close a skating line.

The proposed footprint belongs to our design and is justified against our6–12m/s
skating speeds. It must never be described as a measured THPS level size. Test
line cadence and traversal in a playable native layout before committing to art.
The owner permits cutting old areas; preserve prior progress/content in a
separately loaded City Tour rather than instantiating the sprawling city behind
the new default. Save-domain and runtime-interface migration need an ADR.

## Delivery and acceptance

The level designer owns the revised scaled plan and playable geometry. Root owns
default-world selection and integration; independent QA owns portal/headroom,
save compatibility and actual native-line review. The new default needs several
closed routes spanning different areas with real grind, air, manual and wall
connections. A route diagram or guide count does not pass that gate: an ordinary
input replay must retain one score chain and bank it once, alongside filmed
human-readable approaches and landings.

The [revised working plan](../planning/COMPACT-LEVEL-REPLAN-2026-11-07.md) records
current coordinates, feature contracts, budgets, tickets and verification targets.
Its proposed or unmeasured values stay labelled until implemented and exercised.
Passing the old city's6,600-assertion regression preserves existing behaviour;
it does not certify this unbuilt replacement or AAA fidelity.
