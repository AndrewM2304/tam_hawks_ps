# ADR-029: Named grinds require declared support and solved contacts

23 October 2026. Local continuation from `ef0eb9f`; no cloud service is required. Owner A01 integration, A04 contact geometry, A05 visual support, A09 independent checks. This implements the first bounded part of TRICK-G01, not the complete grind roster.

Keep existing Godot Curve3D/GrindLine travel, native capsule sweeps, input freshness, 65-degree approach gate, balance, gravity and momentum. A travel angle is not a grind style. Retain legacy `rail_grind` on unverified guides; introduce `grind_50_50` and `boardslide` only where the author declares a support datum and the pose/clearance solver can establish it. Nosegrind/5-0, nose/tail slides, lipslide truck-crossing history and style changes remain later slices. Neutral/up/down choose parallel contact; left/right choose signed boardslide. An already perpendicular drawn board can also select boardslide. Snapshot direction and pre-capture board heading before clearing air spin. Do not call a final perpendicular pose a lipslide without crossing history.

GrindLine gains backward-compatible contact metadata: finite world-vertical and tangent-normal offsets, provenance and an explicit verified flag. Original points and gameplay travel do not move. Known authoring helpers declare their actual top/centre/skin convention. World-solid semantic ledges/bars retain generic eligibility: the whole-board envelope fills air between wheels, and centred level contacts can intrude into broad support tops. Component envelopes/conforming edge contacts are a later bounded slice, not permission to ignore obstacles. Radial normal-offset guides require flat paths or consistent straight 3D grade; changing-grade joints remain generic. Unsupported transforms and uncertain edge families retain the generic path. The original board asset supplies hanger underside `(0,-0.103,±0.255)` and central deck underside `(0,-0.014,0)`; these are project dimensions, not THPS constants. A reusable contact pose solves board orientation/translation after balance, slope and yaw. Unsupported neutral geometry may retain generic grinding; a solved but obstructed or oversized named correction must reject rather than bypass clearance through fallback. Two rigid truck contacts need a feasible chord on the real guide; do not stretch the board across a kink or certify an unsupported short rail.

Travel/camera root stays on its signed rail tangent. The board and planted rider follow the solved contact frame. Sideways exit preserves an unscored orientation offset in air; only explicit player spin earns a spin award. Named entry emits one style award and one unchanged authored rail-ID event. Catalog family recognition routes new names to grind audio and encounter rules. Existing score values remain 200 for this first slice; distinct styles may legitimately produce distinct combo identities. Do not emit a compatibility second award.

Fix the reproduced capture overshoot independently of style: swept candidates must fit both current-origin correction<=0.85m and previous-native-sample→final-target<1m (0.99m ceiling). Keep clearance from the actual current body, search later eligible samples/candidates after refusal and retain the existing missed-window tests. Mesh smoothing is not proof of bounded physical movement.

Budget declared before integration: zero new visible meshes/surfaces/draws/primitives/materials/textures or physics bodies. One persistent native board-envelope BoxShape3D/query per skater; at most one extra conservative continuous board-envelope query per existing0.20m native travel substep (at most two per60Hz named-grind tick at the18m/s speed cap), with candidate-entry queries bounded by existing nearby-line search. Reuse contact buffers; no per-tick resource/container construction in the solver. Native intersect_shape uses the previous rigid frame, midpoint of board origins, axiswise translation expansion and 4*r*sin(theta/2) angular size expansion (r=.457m model-origin corner bound). This encloses intermediate corners and may conservatively refuse tight bends. The persistent query-only box is resized synchronously and restored; final endpoint/slow steps use the same gate. Named release retains final solved chord yaw and visual translation through the existing short decay, without changing physical momentum. Shape/query startup memory target<=64KiB per skater. Keep strict native A/B/A p95/p99 envelope and<=1.05×worse-control gates; record p50, long frames, pose/contact CPU, draws/primitives/static/render memory and system load. No map expansion, remote CI, paid API or imported assets/code.

Acquisition remains a bounded discrete contact snap with a capsule sweep and destination board-volume check. The continuous board enclosure applies during active travel, not between the last free-air board and its first contact. Named presentation overrides the old cosmetic entry offset; native rendering interpolation still needs a dedicated intermediate-frame obstruction probe. G01-07 records that investigation without claiming a reproduced visible tunnel or certified continuous acquisition.

Admission requires meaningful pure support/selection/rigid-contact tests, independent mapped-input/native sampling, board-only obstruction negatives, generic fallback, reversed/sloped/bent travel, held-input/exit/pause/reset/bail behavior, audio/encounter and actual route scoring. Run tools/check.sh on frozen final source; save original contact/motion frames and retain rejected evidence. Owner feel, independent visual acceptance, real controllers/exports, fully isolated timing and long-play stutter remain evidence gates.
