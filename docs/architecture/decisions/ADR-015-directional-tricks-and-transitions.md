# ADR-015 — Directional tricks and finite transition skating

- **Date:** 17 October 2026
- **Status:** Accepted implementation; full final regression/render receipts in STATUS. Human Mac/controller/fun acceptance remains open.
- **Scope:** Existing M01 controls/camera, M02 tricks/transition/lips and M06 verification. The owner explicitly prioritizes these over the previous deferred transition plan. No new district or paid dependency.

The owner needs more than one flip/grab/manual, identifiable animation selected by controls, actual quarter/half pipes and classic arcade camera/turning. Primary THPS3/4 instruction manuals and pinned MIT references are inspected in CLASSIC-TRICK-CONTROLS. Mechanics/control grammar inform original code and animation; franchise code, layouts, media and animation assets are not copied. Nollie/switch modifiers and complex trick-editor/chord systems remain outside this slice.

Hold Ollie to crouch/push, release to pop. A short tap retains8.8m/s base impulse; charge adds up to1.2m/s over350ms. Existing120ms request/coyote boundaries remain. The request must physically execute before a career deadline; a release after expiry cannot retroactively launch. Disable/reset flush held charge.

Direction is latched when Flip/Grab/Manual/Grind is pressed, surviving input buffering. Six flips, five held grabs, tail/nose manuals and four lip variants have separate original board/hand/weight poses. Fresh flips can chain only after their clip completes. Held buttons never repeat entries. Air left/right rotates the visible skater and preserves carried horizontal velocity; shoulders are an alternate spin input. Selecting a family consumes its direction briefly so it does not also create an unwanted spin. Landed180-degree multiples retain regular/fakie visual stance; no new strict landing-angle bails are introduced without readable controls.

Manual + Up selects Nose Manual; neutral manual input selects Manual. Optional Up→neutral→Down and reverse gestures latch a manual/nose manual within220ms. Requiring a sampled neutral prevents ordinary direct push/brake reversals from starting an unintended manual. A dedicated manual press exits a gesture-latched manual, including overtime. An existing gesture manual survives entry-disable; fresh entries do not. Both manual variants satisfy Mairi's existing manual challenge without changing IDs/rewards/saves.

Fresh Grind arms a140ms released-press request, or one held seek until the first eligible capture. A rail locks on entry; release sustains it, a second fresh press drops, release-Ollie pops. Capture consumes the held seek, so holding cannot repeatedly recapture. Lip tricks instead require held Grind near actual configured coping reached from a confirmed uphill curve, low lateral speed and a clear capsule path. Left/right balances; release drops inward, release-Ollie pops. A stall awards once, is limited to4s, and normal career overtime remains capped at3s. Supported ramp travel alone never holds a combo.

Three original90-degree cylindrical faces live on the existing Clyde north quay: standalone toe(17,2,64.5), facing halfpipe toes(-26,2,60.5)/(-18,2,60.5). Radius3.2m, width10m, shared2-degree surface samples generate visible mesh and physical collision. Halfpipe centre moved1m north after the protected z67 route's normal arrival tolerance exposed a real deck-side collision; the route now remains unobstructed. Decks/sides/coping and the open flat bottom are real geometry, not a new district or invisible catch surface.

`TransitionRide` retains signed arc/lateral velocity, integrates tangent gravity at240Hz internally, and requires collision against the exact live finite front face at entry and every step. The player sweeps its real normal-aligned capsule in short steps and reconciles actual collision-adjusted movement. Low speed rolls back; a full-speed vertical lip releases its actual upward velocity. Descending front-face re-entry is separate from ordinary walls/backfaces. A blocked move preserves confirmed contact and lost momentum; removed support cannot leave an infinite plane.

Coping support samples slightly inside the final triangle because curve/deck share an exact endpoint. The capsule sweeps to a reached coping target in its existing contact frame, verifies final upright clearance, then changes frame. A stale CharacterBody floor flag from move_and_collide cannot overwrite a subsequent real aerial exit; ordinary ground handling also requires `not airborne`. Unsafe upright overlap uses one safe bail rather than an intersecting ground state. Reset clears every transition/lip/collider state.

Ground turning tightens to2.8→2.2rad/s with100ms smoothing and mild analogue shaping. The lower chase camera uses world-up horizon, a travel-facing reverse hysteresis and actual transition-normal feet/chest/head visibility checks, retaining the previous sphere/orbit obstruction repair. Chosen camera/turn numbers are original tuning values, not extracted commercial constants. Final physical controller feel and target frame times remain evidence gates.
