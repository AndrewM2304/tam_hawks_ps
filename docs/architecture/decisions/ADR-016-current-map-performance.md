# ADR-016 — Current-map rendering and frame consistency

- Date: 17 October 2026
- Status: Implemented; local measurements/captures, 1,701/0 ordinary regression, unsigned native audits and Linux startup complete; exact receipts in STATUS. Target-hardware/final-art acceptance open.
- Scope: Existing M06-004/M01-005/M05-001/M06-007 and release checks. No map expansion, paid dependency or unattended work.

The owner reports persistent stuttering and prioritizes performance. A fresh source-stable six-view baseline measures 1,930 median Exchange draws and 1,617 Barras draws at 1280×800 on Linux software rendering. Those are current workload observations, not Apple M5 timings. Camera FOV alone cannot resolve repeated submission, pixel shading, per-frame CPU work or the mismatch between physics and rendered motion. Keep current gameplay framing while reducing avoidable work.

Use explicit static author records for opaque architectural MultiMeshes. Finalize after all world dressing and parent/threshold changes. Share only identical mesh/material/full render state in actual instance-position cells, preserving transforms/channels and all physics. Require a strict per-family batch-count saving. Measure 32 m/48 m/64 m candidates in eight unchanged world-only views, validating authored and live renderer records after upload warmup with consuming tolerant multiset checks. Integer-quantized hash identity is diagnostic, not the geometry-preservation gate. Select 64 m after accepted comparisons: it reduces the busiest-view submissions most, with the documented Central tradeoff (567→591 draws). All 17,742 records survive and physics remains unchanged.

Create occluders only inset inside explicitly registered closed opaque facade boxes. Replaced/hidden originals, transparent walls, porticos, aisles, canopies and bridges cannot become blockers. Compare native CPU occlusion separately. Although counters fall using 23 contained facade occluders, exact off/on/off-repeat images expose missing distant facade planes. Native occlusion remains disabled; containment and fewer draws do not establish visual correctness. The 64 m batching-only Barras change is 1,284→1,015 draws. Central and increased batch-bound primitive submission remain explicit tradeoffs. Gameplay/collision stays active outside the view; no per-district load/unload triggers or traversal-time rebatching are introduced.

Cache event-derived goal/unlock snapshots using a public exact presentation revision and retain defensive ownership. Avoid unchanged HUD/settings copies, prompt formatting and text layout. Do not throttle balance feedback or authoritative physics. The measured microbenchmark saves about 0.18–0.23 ms per refresh on this host; it cannot alone certify a frame-rate improvement.

Enable native physics interpolation for the Skater and its physics-driven poses. Main and the per-render world-space camera opt out. Chase follows the displayed interpolated rider and support axis, while collision/camera queries retain real physics space. Restart/recovery clear histories, with a first-update fallback for Godot's same-frame cache. Controls, forces, scoring and physical trajectories remain unchanged. This addresses stepping between render frames; shader/CPU/GPU stalls need separate evidence.

Add saved Performance/Balanced/High choices without changing save version or existing progress. Balanced retains native 3D scale, 220 m far plane, 2× MSAA and 45 m shadows. High retains the previous 75 m shadows. Performance uses 0.75 3D scale, 220 m far plane, 30 m shadows and no MSAA; native UI resolution and all simulation remain. A 150 m Performance candidate clipped City Hall while leaving its dome visible, so every preset retains 220 m until selective silhouette/detail LOD exists. The short minimum clear camera arm permits a 0.20 m near plane instead of 0.08 m, improving depth precision for layered facades without changing FOV or follow distance. Its practical flicker benefit needs Mac motion review.

Add a bounded native-render moving diagnostic with mapped controls, no per-tick pose/velocity correction, monotonic post-draw CSV, CPU/physics/submission counters, progress gates and source/artifact hashes. Fixed-clock screenshots remain presentation evidence, not performance measurements. Required ordinary regressions, cold-source launch, actual UI/chase images and matched after timing/counters precede completion. Keep physical Mac/DualSense feel, ten-minute 1080p p50/p95/p99, final art and unsigned export acceptance explicit.

Commit/push completed tasks and recoverable source checkpoints throughout the session. Preserve all 75 full backlog records, ten goals, three side acts and exact resume steps.
