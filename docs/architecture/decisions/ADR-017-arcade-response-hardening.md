# ADR-017 — Weighted jumps, finite vert returns and reachable edges

18 October 2026. Authorized by the owner's reported floaty motion, failed pipe returns, weak grind snapping and request for a complete existing-map audit. Original implementation informed by the inspected sources in [ARCADE-RESPONSE-2026-10](../../research/ARCADE-RESPONSE-2026-10.md); no new upstream code or assets are copied.

Free flight uses gravity 34 m/s². Release-Ollie ranges from 8.0 to 11.3 m/s after a deliberate 250 ms crouch. Native measured tap/charged flights are approximately 1.02/1.98 m high and 0.48/0.68 seconds airborne, versus 1.85/2.37 m and 0.82/0.92 seconds in the baseline fixtures. Contact gravity remains 22 for the authored streets/transitions; this is deliberately state-specific arcade tuning. No universal real-world gravity claim follows. Flip animation lasts 320 ms and ground steering response takes 55 ms; neither changes camera sensitivity into a physical force.

A wall pop raises upward speed to at least the requested pop, without adding a second full impulse to existing ascent. Steep upward departures retain only their finite live feature, footprint and non-scoring return yaw. Automatic turning follows the shortest rotation toward the downhill direction; actual velocity remains carried. Downhill transition contact faces travel, allowing ordinary push to continue across the flat instead of cancelling negative speed against an old uphill heading. Contact pose includes lateral velocity. There is no infinite attachment plane or scored automatic 180.

Changing an aligned capsule upright requires a real outward sweep and a clear upright volume, bounded to 0.34 m. Failed clearance bails without awarding an Ollie. Transition admission/departure clears stale street coyote eligibility; rail admission clears a previous vert assist. Repeated immediate taps cannot reuse the old contact. Real mapped single/double wall pops and alternating half-pipe cycles are regressions, not just helper arithmetic.

Deliberate grind admission permits 0.85 m total snap, 0.60 m above / 0.14 m below, and a 65° horizontal approach. A fresh press has 180 ms buffering; a held approach still seeks only one capture. Collision sweeps reject blocked paths. Angled catches retain at least 90% of incoming horizontal speed without turning falling speed into a flat-rail boost. Physics locks immediately; a 45 ms visual offset recovery softens the catch. Explicit closed copings wrap at their authored seam; open rails still release at endpoints. An 8 m startup spatial index excludes distant curves while retaining the full 1 m swept capture window.

Existing selected physical ledges, counters, benches, bridge bars and kerbs gain semantic tracks; flush terrain seams and arbitrary decorative/high/outside edges do not become invisible rails. Four formerly art-only reachable benches receive matching slat/frame collision. Exact surface queries follow collision triangles and consistent seam ownership; they do not create ground outside the authored mesh. The 48 routes, career/save identities and explicit water hazard remain authoritative.

This decision does not close human Mac/DualSense feel, all aerial access, final art, or performance acceptance. Required ordinary checks, native mapped-input play, independent support probes and real rendered review remain separate evidence. New rendering must pass declared costs and matched timing gates; no paid service or map expansion is authorized by this decision.
