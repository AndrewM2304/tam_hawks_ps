# ADR-032: playtest station enclosure and existing-park pocket direction

Date: 25 October 2026. Status: **Accepted planning direction; not implemented**.

The owner supplied the completed 24 October debug session and asked for tickets
only, for later cloud implementation. Capture 23's tentative train suggestion
is clarified as an enclosed station building at the map edge, with trains passing
through a tunnel. Capture 24 suggests a pond, ducks, grindable perimeter and a
water bail in existing empty Kelvingrove grass. Other notes ask for purposeful
connected skating lines rather than arbitrary ramps, walls and dead spaces.

Keep the existing six-area map and skating-first roadmap. Plan original enclosed
station massing and a nonplayable railway corridor/tunnel, with scenic train
motion concealed at entry/exit. Preserve safe station/civic/park passages and
all stable route/goal/save identities. This does not add a playable railway,
traffic/skitching system, copied station plan or required train hazard.

Plan the pond inside the recorded existing park pocket, with a dry bypass,
physical grind contact and water entry routed through the existing bail/recovery
lifecycle. Use modest scenic ducks rather than an animal simulation. Resolve
ground support, recovery and route safety before adding visual complexity.

Train/pond/furniture construction must declare its own bounded performance
budget, retain ordinary offscreen gameplay and pass the existing stutter gates
before rendering expansion. Reuse built-in Godot facilities/shared resources,
original or compatible free assets with source/licence records. Research Glasgow
references before detailed station art; no unverified factual reconstruction claim.
No paid service, copied branding/music/layout or new district is authorized here.

The owner has provided the creative direction; routine implementation choices
do not need another approval. Native visual/feel, physical hardware and export
acceptance remain evidence gates. Planning does not claim these gates have passed.
