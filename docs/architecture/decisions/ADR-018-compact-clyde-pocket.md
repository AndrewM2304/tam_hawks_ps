# ADR-018 — Compact the existing Clyde feature groups

18 October 2026. Accepted for implementation following the owner's approval of the strict density review. Final gameplay/render/performance admission is separate and belongs in current STATUS.

The previous Clyde used300m quays, bridges133m apart and a560×90m reachable landscape around a small Print Yard. The [strict review](../../levels/STRICT-DENSITY-REVIEW.md) found excessive transport and accessible scenery. The owner approved proceeding with the [ordered density tasks](../../planning/LEVEL-DENSITY-NEXT.md).

Implement one compact river pocket before moving the park or core arenas. The [coordinate contract](../../levels/COMPACT-CLYDE-CONTRACT.md) defines170m north quay,115m south quay/yard,45m crossing separation, preserved38m river breadth and native8m optional bridge opening. Move the complete yard, pipe, bridge art, physical support, rail snapshots, query functions, gap anchors and recovery positions together. Use one shared authored layout module. Preserve career/save/encounter/gap identities and skating-force/trick contracts; route coordinates and redundant scenery counts may change intentionally.

Keep the half-pipe dimensions. The quarter is rotated toward the east at(−15,2,62), with a landward through-lane atZ69. Earlier candidate positions and theZ74 lane are rejected: their deck or the bridge's solid handrails obstructed the safe approach/through-route. Preserve those failures as evidence instead of widening the rider or capture envelopes.

The former south lawn is removed from both physics and published support. Retain only the useful landing/yard/return terrain and close dry ends with visible3m solid property walls using existing stone. These replace see-through fencing at the cut world edge and reduce shapes/instances. Keep the river as an explicit readable water hazard with supported local recovery. The existing Carlton-inspired row is shortened, with matching physical bounds. Do not add a hidden catch floor, new district, paid asset, texture or shader.

Keep retained yard ground flush atY2. The rejected1.8m floor exposed a20cm ribbon lip that stopped a real-input return. Remove the duplicate ribbon display while retaining its collision, support and published route. Behind the raised north-bank decks, replace only the existing west landward boundary span endingX−15/Z55.2 with10m solid masonry (topY12). Its former3m fence ended atY5, below the5.2m decks. This one-box wall reuses an existing material, preserves both city port openings and covers the conservative unpumped18m/s lip-apex estimate nearY10; arbitrary stacked-trick containment is not claimed.

The bent city approaches keep real16m-wide support. Native polygon subtraction trims only their displayed triangles against the quay so shared asphalt/paving surfaces do not flicker. Publish the real intermediate diagonal-plane waypoint on the east grade; keep query geometry matched to its physical triangles. Selected embankment grind spans respect moved bridge mouths and the south wall's local origin.

The first ordinary chase captures still exposed grey missing ground through the approach/end ironwork. Keep its actual graded convex collision and replace bar/plinth displays with one opaque stone ArrayMesh per existing body: four approach edges, two north-quay ends and two ordinary landward spans. Generate all six visible faces from the enabled collision's exact eight corners, with face normals/UVs/tangents; do not fill bent approaches with an axis-aligned box. This reduces displayed bar work without new materials, textures, bodies or dynamic behavior. The raised west span and south walls retain their optimized straight solid boxes. The initial route map also depicted the safe crossing as a disconnected spur. Extend the existing published yard-loop metadata to its actual safe-bridge endpoint along the supported south return; preserve its ID and physical ribbon. Both defects require refreshed actual captures, not a declaration that containment alone fixes composition.

Require real-input captures/tricks/manuals/banking and connected returns, independent rider-size geometry/support checks, full required regression, actual chase/map/overview images and matched rendering measurements. Compact placement can increase simultaneous visibility; lower total geometry alone is not a frame-time pass. The current rejected art previews remainOFF. Human fun, physical DualSense and Apple M5 timing remain open.
