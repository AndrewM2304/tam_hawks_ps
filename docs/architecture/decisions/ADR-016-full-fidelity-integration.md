# ADR-016: Connected full skating fidelity and useful whole-map features

Accepted for implementation 24 October 2026. Validation remains in progress in
[STATUS](../../STATUS.md). The owner explicitly rejected stopping after ADR-015's
bounded core checkpoint. This extends its engineering scope; it does not certify

## Native process and animation

Boneless and No Comply require a real front-foot support ray before admission.
The .22/.16-second preparation retains a local anchor on the actual supporting
body, including moving carts. One native movement owns each preparation frame;
the launch frame performs one pop movement. Failed support cannot receive a
Boneless impulse or score. The rig uses real sole/palm markers and catches the
deck after deliberate separation; ordinary stance agrees with signed travel.

Bail captures the interrupted world-space pose before clearing trick state.
Combo loss happens immediately once; native slide/fall and articulated recovery
follow. Airborne fall cannot advance to getup. Recovery uses actual supported
clearance or a bounded faded safe return. Fade continues after the results screen
disables controls. Pause freezes both native action and presentation.

CharacterBody support velocity is separate from skater-relative signed speed.
Native carrying and leave momentum apply once. Teleport/restart flushes cached
platform support on the next real move; legitimate in-place getup retains it.

## Connected repertoire

The catalog has 78 stable definitions, with 24 flip stages (eight bases × single,
double, triple), eight grabs, ten grind actions, eleven manuals, nine lip styles,
six original specials and existing core actions. Definitions alone are insufficient:
actual Main input/catch/score checks activate every new repertoire entry.

Flip upgrades replace one accepted entry and interpolate from the current rotation
without a pose reset. Grab tweaks add a bounded bonus to the held entry. Manual
and lip extensions require fresh actions and cooldown. Rail Pivot turns physical
facing 180 degrees, reverses relative rail facing and costs 15% speed while retaining
actual travel; it scores once per accepted pivot. Accepted landing Revert consumes
its queued action so it cannot replay as an unrelated later extension.

Six special motions share the existing two career unlocks; no save migration is
needed. Completed body/board rotation commits to actual caught stance. Rotation
bonuses do not manufacture multiplier entries. Incomplete catches retain real
landing/bail eligibility. Player guide and interfaces specify commands and values.

## Curved terrain and usable surfaces

The production transition interface now supports circular bowls, connected halfpipe
faces and actual spine transfers. Bowl lateral motion uses surface metric and
parallel transport. Descending associated air may acquire another eligible native
face. Lip poses use the top of real steel coping and upright capsule clearance;
face normal and coping tangent remain separate from standing support normal.

World infill adds linked banks/manual decks/ledges in all six existing places,
graded park ledges and climbing rails. Current surface inventory and exclusions
are in the [full audit](../../levels/FULL-FIDELITY-LEVEL-AUDIT.md). Route proximity
is one bounded density measure; native approach/capture and ordinary input lines
provide separate evidence. Protected career, encounter, water and camera paths
remain authoritative. No invisible catch floor or dense prop scatter substitutes
for meaningful supported routes.

## Optional activities and skitch

One FeatureHub coordinates the reversible service gate, physical cone stunt,
two-route park lap and original low-speed quay cart. The two new challenges join
the existing nearest-offer arbitration; the gate remains an independent interaction.
All observe real player events, pause/reset/finish correctly and remain run-local.
No points, career goals, save fields or unlock identities are added.

The service gate is a real moving body with a player sensor and permanent bypass.
The route map follows actual enabled state. Cone success requires its full swept
hull through the native ring, real raft contact, player landing and ordinary bank.
Both park routes require ordered native traversal, real pop/landing and return.

The cart's native chassis/cab/four wheels match visible geometry. It reports actual
velocity and moves before the skater. A separate front pedestrian sensor avoids
mutual rider/platform collision feedback. Skitch uses native movement, not position
snaps: pending reach has no hand pose or score; actual bar catch admits one entry,
two palm contacts, bounded balance/tow and ordinary charged release momentum.

New adult offer actors reuse the inspected licensed anatomy, garments and footwear.
No new paid asset, dependency, API, resource, billing change or publication occurs.
