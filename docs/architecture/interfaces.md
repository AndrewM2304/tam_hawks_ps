# Runtime interfaces

Updated 17 October 2026 against the current implementation. Godot 4.6.3, Compatibility renderer, native GDScript, one attributed MIT rail-geometry component, original world/garment geometry and Blender-exported GLB board, CC0 cropped MakeHuman anatomical meshes, two attributed CC0 material sets, and local audio. Runtime has no network service or paid dependency. This describes enabled behavior, not human acceptance or target-Mac certification.

Coordinates use +Y up and local -Z forward. The board-contact origin starts at `(-22, 0.8, 28)` and settles onto Y=0. Six connected, originally authored places comprise the map: Royal Exchange, George Square, Central Station, the Barras, Clyde Quays and Kelvingrove Park. Exchange gallery is 2 m above the lower fountain court; George is 4 m with a 6 m civic terrace; Central concourse 2 m with 5 m platforms; Barras loading terrace 1.5 m. The river lies south, the hilly park northwest. These are compressed game coordinates, not surveyed Glasgow street geometry. Generic player recovery catches Y below -8 or absolute X/Z above 400; the world also supplies a local safe-quay return for river falls.

## Input and profiles

`game/core/input_setup.gd` registers the following defaults with action deadzone 0.2. Gamepad events use device -1, so they accept any connected controller. Physical DualSense mapping, trigger ergonomics and wired/Bluetooth behavior remain hardware checks.

| Actions | Keyboard | Standard DualSense candidate |
|---|---|---|
| `push`, `brake` | W/up, S/down | Left stick up/down |
| `steer_left`, `steer_right` | A/left, D/right | Left stick left/right |
| `ollie`, `flip`, `grab` | Space, J, K | Cross, Square, Circle |
| `grind`, `manual` | L, M | Triangle, L2 |
| `revert` | C | R2 |
| `spin_left`, `spin_right` | Q, E | L1, R1 |
| `special_one`, `special_two` | U, I | D-pad up/right |
| `interact`, `goals` | X, Tab | D-pad down, Create |
| `restart`, `pause`, `free_skate` | R, Escape, F | R3, Options, touchpad |

`game/core/input_profile.gd` owns validated overrides. `binding_snapshot()` supplies readable live bindings; `rebind(action,event)` returns `{ok,error,bindings}`; `apply_saved(bindings)` validates the complete candidate before changing InputMap; `reset_defaults()` restores defaults. Overrides contain only permitted physical key/button/axis fields. Duplicate bindings, unknown actions, unsupported axes, modifier/echo keys and weak axis capture are rejected. Analog capture requires magnitude 0.5. Keyboard and controller overrides are separate; Godot `ui_*` actions are preserved. Career settings persist accepted binding dictionaries. A controller disconnect pauses active gameplay; keyboard fallback remains available.

## Skater and presentation

`game/player/skater.gd` extends `CharacterBody3D`. Public state includes `speed`, `airborne`, `spawn_position`, `grinding`, `grind_balance`, `manual_active`, `manual_balance`, `revert_remaining`, `special_name`, `active_flip_id`, `active_grab_id`, `manual_id`, `lip_id`, `lip_active`, `lip_balance`, `transition_active` and `transition_normal`. Signals are `grind_captured(rail_id)`, `trick_performed(name,points)`, `landed(clean)`, `bailed()`, `combo_link_started()` and `combo_link_ended()`.

`reset_skater()` restores motion and all transient trick/visual state. `recover_to(safe_position)` emits one bail and resets at the supplied finite world position, then restores the original run spawn so Restart still starts in the city. Main invokes it when `world.get_recovery_position` returns a finite water recovery point; banked score survives and pending tricks/gap tracking clear. `set_controls_enabled(false)` stops horizontal movement and an active manual; `set_trick_entries_enabled(false)` rejects new launches/tricks/captures/manuals/reverts/specials while allowing an existing rail/manual/lip to resolve during bounded overtime. Pausable node processing freezes physics and visual timers. Actual landings emit once; visible spins remain independent of flight velocity, 180-degree multiples retain visual stance, and ordinary heading-only landings remain forgiving.

Native physics interpolation is enabled project-wide at the existing 60Hz simulation rate. Main explicitly opts out before constructing children; Skater opts in and its independent, per-render `ChaseCamera` opts out. During render updates, `ChaseCamera._follow_transform` follows `get_global_transform_interpolated()` for the rider and contact-aligned `BodyCollision`; physics callers and the first update after a chase reset use the current exact transform. The displayed contact axis supplies body-framing points while the horizon remains world-up. Reset/recovery primes and resets interpolation history after restoring the rider and descendant poses, preventing a return from blending through the old location. Interpolation changes presentation, not collision or trick timing. The camera near plane is 0.20m; profile-dependent far planes are below.

Ground motion projects onto the current floor. Slope acceleration precedes deliberate braking so a stopped brake holds a hill; disabled controls retain zero horizontal speed. Air carries collision-adjusted horizontal velocity without stick-induced curvature; the same left/right input now rotates the visible skater. Ground speed uses capped actual travel velocity to avoid artificial downhill braking. The 56° floor classification supports ordinary banks; eligible near-vertical faces use the separate finite transition state below.

- `set_grind_lines(lines)` snapshots world-space authored tracks. Fresh buffered or first-held-seek eligible air capture uses `GrindRide`; capsule sweeps reject scenery obstruction. Point capture precedes movement; `start_swept` also samples the completed actual move at intervals no greater than 0.10m, rejecting travel over 1m. Historical samples retain the same admission envelope and check clearance from the actual current origin, using actual collision-adjusted velocity. A released press is buffered for 140ms; one held seek remains armed until its first capture. Capture consumes both. The ride locks after release and a second fresh press drops; hold/release Ollie pops out. Held input cannot recapture indefinitely. Entry awards 200 once and resolves prior spin without a landing. Release preserves signed tangent momentum, and recapture waits 0.35 seconds.
- Fresh flip/grab requests have a consumable 0.12-second buffer into eligible airtime. Reset, disable, overtime and capture flush requests; landing clears unused air requests. A held input cannot repeat an award. Manual accepts a fresh eligible supported press, a held request within 0.12 seconds before landing, or a neutral-separated Up/Down gesture within 0.22 seconds, speed magnitude at least 2 m/s and a 0.35-second exit cooldown. An at-rest rejected press cannot become eligible merely by holding it while accelerating. Reverse manual travel is supported. Up+Manual selects Nose Manual; gesture manuals stay latched until a dedicated manual press, ollie, low speed or bail. Existing gesture holds survive entry-disable while new entries are refused. It coasts while push/brake correct fore/aft balance and left/right still steer. Entry awards 100 once and emits link-start before scoring. Release/insufficient speed emits link-end once; an actual ollie emits link-start and continues airtime. Losing support clears manual without a false ground-end. Absolute balance 1 bails.
- Revert requires fresh grounded input within `revert_remaining` after actual air landing, cannot occur during a manual/rail, and is once per landing. It costs 15% speed, awards 150 and animates a cosmetic 180° stance change over 0.18 seconds. It emits no continuation event and never resets the score deadline. `revert_remaining` is input eligibility; it is not the authoritative banking timer.
- `try_special(id) -> bool` accepts only the two catalog specials during eligible unpaused airtime, once per actual air segment. It emits the catalog award only on success. Main checks career unlock and meter first, and consumes the meter only after `true`. Original 0.65-second board-spin/body-tuck animations do not rotate the collision shape or change travel velocity.

`set_presentation_settings(sensitivity,reduced_motion)` clamps camera-follow sensitivity to 0.5–2 and removes speed-dependent camera distance/FOV when reduced motion is enabled. `set_skater_color(Color)` changes the shared shirt material. The presentation helpers `game/player/skater_visual.gd` and `skater_garments.gd` build an original ten-bone Skeleton3D with continuous skinned jacket/trousers, structured shoes, detailed board and analytic action poses. A verified CC0 MakeHuman-derived adult head, scalp and five-finger hands replace the original geometric face/hands; no full body model, MakeHuman program or skin texture is shipped. The pose snapshot includes speed, airborne/grab/flip/manual/grind/push/brake, steering and balance, revert time, special ID/progress. Feet follow the deck and grabs target its edge. Tenement Tumble rotates the visible rider/board around a body-height pivot, without moving the collision origin. Keep `SkaterVisual`, `Board`, `Rider`, and `BodyCollision` intact; the controller aligns the collision capsule during supported transitions and restores it when clear. Final character quality and human animation acceptance remain open.

## Score and combo resolution

`game/scoring/score_session.gd` is the authoritative `RefCounted` score/deadline model. It exposes `total_score`, `combo_base`, `combo_multiplier`, `best_combo`, `last_trick`, `last_banked`, `trick_history`, `pending_landing` and `landing_remaining`.

`add_trick(name,points)` adds repeat-degraded points; identity deliberately remains the display name, including distinct spin labels. `get_combo_value()` previews the capped multiplier. `begin_landing()` opens a single **0.35-second** pending bank only for a nonempty combo; duplicates cannot extend it. `continue_combo()` clears pending banking for a valid movement link. `tick(delta)` consumes active-play time and returns a bank exactly once on expiry. Adding a trick, including Revert, does not refresh or cancel the timer. `bank_combo()`, `drop_combo()` and `reset()` clear all pending deadline and repetition state.

Per-event values are capped at 1,000,000; base, multiplied combo and total records saturate at 2,147,483,647. Repeats halve to a sixteenth and distinct names multiply up to 8. Career records separately clamp to their save-model bound of 2,000,000,000.

Main connects player link-start to `continue_combo`, link-end/clean landing to `begin_landing`, and bail to `drop_combo`. While physical air, rail or manual remains active it calls `continue_combo`; ordinary loss of support therefore sustains an existing combo without inventing an Ollie award. Clean ground travel banks after the remaining window. The full ordering and overtime rules are in [ADR-006](decisions/ADR-006-trick-state-and-input.md).

## Rail helpers and catalog

The adapted MIT `game/tricks/grind_line.gd` owns a world-space `Curve3D`. Constructor `(id,world_points,kind)` and `from_local_points(id,points,transform,kind)` snapshot geometry. `is_valid` rejects invalid/collapsed lines; `point_at(distance)` clamps endpoints; `dir_at(distance)` returns a full 3D tangent; `closest(position)` returns `{dist,point,gap}` or `{}`. Units are world metres. Rebuild after authoring transforms change. Retain its complete upstream notice.

Original `game/tricks/grind_ride.gd` owns signed progress, speed and balance. `start(lines,from,velocity,clearance)` selects an eligible clear candidate; `step(delta,balance_input)` returns `riding`, `end`, `bail` or `slow`. `position()` and `travel_velocity()` retain the release sample; `stop()` deactivates the ride. The player supplies collision sweeps, input gating and scoring. See [GRINDING](../testing/GRINDING.md) for tuning and actual authored tracks.

`game/tricks/trick_catalog.gd` provides copy-returning definitions, stable IDs, known-state/transition guards and repeat arithmetic. Structural permission alone never proves physical eligibility, unlocks, charge or input freshness. Manual, revert, both specials and authored gaps are now wired to runtime systems; catalog presence remains insufficient to enable any later addition.

## World, gaps and objective markers

`game/levels/glasgow_square.gd` owns core city collision, terraced courts, graded streets, stairs, fountain coping, authored rails, goal/gap definitions, cone interaction and NPC locations. `core_terrain.gd` builds matching mesh/collision patches. `outdoor_routes.gd` adds Clyde quays, an eight-metre bridge opening with a continuous alternative, and rolling park terrain with paved climbs. `city_art.gd` dresses that layout with original materials/facades/landmark silhouettes and NPC figures. `get_spawn_position()`, `get_location_name(position)`, `get_district_id(position)` and `get_district_checkpoints()` expose navigation data for six areas. `get_ground_height(world_position)` returns highest authored terrain height or NAN outside authored terrain; props/roofs are not terrain. `get_support_height(world_position,max_step_up=1.0)` returns the highest authored terrain support at or below the supplied height plus the step tolerance, allowing a lower station passage beneath an upper deck. Actual ray/capsule checks remain the collision evidence. `support_probe` metadata on core graded bodies supplies a world-space surface-center point for local collision checks without confusing legitimate stacked routes. `get_route_definitions()` returns `{id,label,points,requires_jump}` with ordered world-space ground points. `get_recovery_position(position)` returns a safe local quay spawn or `Vector3.INF` when no hazard applies. `get_grind_lines()` returns fresh transformed track snapshots; ordinary scenery is layer 1 and rail props layer 2/mask 0, so unacquired rails do not obstruct normal travel or award tricks. `activate_cone()`/`reset_interactions()` manage the local visual interaction; `get_npc_positions()` supports nearby audio barks.

`get_gap_definitions()` returns `{id,name,start,end,radius,points,airborne_required}` in world space. Core routes are Gallery Steps (300), Mind The Gap (600) and Market Loading Steps (350), retaining their existing IDs; outdoor gap definitions extend the set. The Clyde transfer awards a real swept, airborne crossing of the bridge opening. `game/tricks/gap_tracker.gd` validates/snapshots definitions and `update(position,airborne,delta)` returns `{id,name,points}` for ordered swept crossings within 4 seconds. Reverse travel, invalid data, overlapping triggers, teleport steps over 20 m and missing required airtime reject. A completed/failed route locks until the rider leaves both triggers. First update only establishes position. Main resets the tracker after bail/restart, awards named points into the combo and records the career event; a grounded gap opens ordinary pending banking.

`get_goal_markers()` supplies `{id,kind,position,radius}` definitions. `game/progression/objective_tracker.gd` renders markers, `poll(position,interact)` returns each nearby event once per run, `nearby_prompt()` exposes content for the HUD's live binding prompt, `collected_count(kind)` reports the actual collected count, and `reset_run()` restores markers. District visits use world regions and do not create collectible orbs. The cone needs deliberate interaction; letters/market items/recording collect by proximity. `reduced_motion` disables marker bob/rotation. Location thresholds and marker radii are gameplay authoring, not verified real-world frontage geometry.

## Career and local persistence

`game/progression/career_progress.gd` (`CareerProgress`) owns ten goals: three banked-score targets (1,000/5,000/15,000), one 3,000-point combo, HAWKS letters, cone duty, Central gap, three market items, four districts and the hidden recording. `begin_run(free_skate)` resets run-local progress; `record_score(total,best_combo)`, `record_event(kind,id)` and `finish_run(total,best_combo)` return newly completed goal IDs. Only an active career run accepts progress; free skate does not advance career records/unlocks. `goals_snapshot()` and `unlocks()` return UI data. Score goals grant orange/Clyde Carousel at First shift, purple at Some shift, and Tenement Tumble at One long line. Blue is always available.

`presentation_revision() -> int` identifies changes to goal/unlock presentation only. It compares run score/combo, completed IDs and collection values exactly against owned previous copies, so public events, reset/load and direct fixture edits are noticed without hash-only invalidation. Main caches defensive `goals_snapshot()`/`unlocks()` results by Career object identity and revision. Returned snapshots remain independent of career source data; settings and records update separately and do not depend on this revision.

`load_profile(path)`/`save_profile(path)` default to `user://career.json`, schema version 1, with validated settings and a verified backup/temp replacement path. A known version-0 schema migrates; unreadable/newer data is preserved and automatic overwrite is blocked. `recover_profile(path)` is an explicit local reset API that archives existing files first; main does not invoke it automatically. Failures leave session play available with save status. Script runners disable persistence by default, and integration tests set it off explicitly.

`game/tricks/special_meter.gd` earns charge only from resolved bank events: points/25, at most 50 units per bank, capped at 100. `consume()` succeeds only at full charge and resets to zero; restarting resets it. Free skate bypasses career-special unlock gating but still needs charge and player eligibility. Rejected requests spend nothing.

## Main, HUD, audio and settings

`game/main.gd` owns component creation, event wiring, 120-second career runs, free skate, pause/results, objective polling and saves. At expiry it rejects new trick links and allows an existing air/rail/manual/pending-ground combo at most 3 seconds to resolve; the deadline cannot be extended. Clean banking routes through `_on_combo_banked` for goals, special charge, feedback and saving. Hard expiry/bail drops unbanked points. Restart clears per-run state and preserves career data.

`game/ui/hud.gd` is a CanvasLayer receiving `update_display(snapshot)`: score/combo/history, timers, speed, manual/rail balance, landing/revert windows, location, mode/results, goals/unlocks/records, special charge, settings, captions and save/interaction status. `save_warning` is persistent failure information, separate from ordinary `save_status`; timed objective notifications have their own area alongside live combo feedback. Signals include restart, pause, free skate, `start_requested(free_skate)`, `settings_changed(values)`, `cosmetic_selected(id)` and UI feedback. Title/settings/controls/goals/pause/results and City map & routes pages are enabled, with nested return navigation and focus restoration. Live binding snapshots drive device-specific remap and interaction prompts. `set_map_data(checkpoints,routes)` supplies the route diagram from actual world coordinates. `show_map()` opens it through the existing paused subpage stack; the arrow shows snapshot position/heading, brighter route segments indicate greater height and dashed orange paths indicate jumps. Pause and Goals expose the map without adding another input action.

Main constructs each encounter status once per HUD refresh and reuses it for challenge/prompt selection. HUD keeps detached mutable nested state/settings, reuses equal owned snapshots, and caches binding text, score fitting and unchanged feedback layout. It skips equal presentation only when device, viewport, authoritative settings and required menu transition also agree. Explicit refreshes still reconcile pause/results immediately; balance markers update in the same call. No presentation throttle changes the 60Hz gameplay loop. [HUD-EFFICIENCY](../testing/HUD-EFFICIENCY.md) records the bounded CPU probe and ownership checks.

`apply_settings(settings,persist)` validates/clamps bindings, master/SFX/ambience volumes, captions, reduced motion, UI scale 1–1.4, sensitivity 0.5–2, graphics quality and earned cosmetics. `select_cosmetic(id,persist)` rejects locked choices. `game/audio/skate_audio.gd` exposes `set_motion`, `set_mix`, `set_surface`, `play_event`, `play_bark`, `set_paused` and a caption signal. Original local synthesis supplies rolling/grinding/events/district beds; generated bark files have separate recorded provenance. No runtime voice/network service is used. Main reads upward floor contacts and their `surface_audio` metadata: asphalt connectors, metal ramps, stone fallback. Air and grinds do not change the last floor selection; audio motion gates their wheel loop. Run completion can trigger the nearby NPC's cooldown-protected “again” line.

`game/core/graphics_quality.gd` exposes `normalized(value) -> String`, returning `balanced` for an unknown/non-string runtime request; `profile(value) -> Dictionary`, returning an isolated scalar profile; and `apply(viewport,skater,world,value) -> String`, applying and returning the normalized ID. The saved `graphics_quality` setting defaults to `balanced`; older saves without the key retain that default, while profile validation rejects invalid stored IDs. ## Current render-resource budget

After world construction, `RenderBudget.apply_static_decor(world)` shares exactly equal built-in PrimitiveMesh resources using all stored properties and material identity. It changes no instance transform, collision or draw distance; imported/procedural ArrayMeshes and scripted/local-to-scene resources remain with their author. Its optional tree partition argument stays off after the matched 48m/96m candidates regressed close-park submissions. See ADR-014 and RENDER-EFFICIENCY-REVIEW for that earlier resource experiment and its evidence limits.

`StaticBatches.register(source,transforms,colors,custom_data) -> bool` separately grants explicit permission to merge immutable architectural MultiMeshes and stores owned authored CPU instance records. Registration promises that source geometry, render state and ancestor transforms/visibility remain static after finalization; runtime animation, trees, gameplay nodes and unregistered visuals are outside that permission. `authored_transforms(source)` and `snapshot(world)` return CPU preservation records; headless GPU-buffer readback is not authoritative.

World startup calls `StaticBatches.finalize(world,cell_size=64.0) -> Dictionary` after primitive sharing. It groups eligible exact mesh/material/render-state families into spatial cells only when the family requires strictly fewer batches, validates staged instance/channel/render-state preservation, then frees the replaced sources. Authors must not keep those source nodes for later movement or visibility changes. Scripted/animated/dynamic/transparent or unsupported render-state sources are rejected by eligibility guards. Finalization is startup-only, not a recurring per-frame traversal; its report is `world.static_batch_report`.

`CityVisibility.register_closed_facade(body)` explicitly marks a permanently closed static facade. `build(world) -> int` creates inset `BoxOccluder3D` instances only from its visible opaque BoxMesh wall and records built ownership; the world exposes `solid_wall_occluders`. Open porticos, halls, canopies, bridges and whole-district bounds must not be registered as closed walls. `GraphicsQuality.apply` leaves viewport occlusion disabled for all three profiles: actual off/on/off-repeat captures exposed missing distant wall planes despite contained boxes. The occluders remain diagnostic candidates; owner-surface protection was insufficient. Frustum/distance/occlusion culling reduces submitted visuals while world resources and gameplay remain loaded. [PERFORMANCE](../testing/PERFORMANCE.md) records the current production comparisons; no FPS result follows from the API alone.

Live area-name HUD text is removed; physical signs, map/pause context and quiet career visit tracking remain.

## Directional trick and transition contracts

ADR-015 owns the current control migration. `DirectionalTricks.select(category,direction)` returns an isolated catalogue definition for flip/grab/manual/lip, with direction X left/right and Y up/down at the fresh press. Requests store that definition; delayed eligibility never rereads direction. `TrickAnimation.board_angles` and `grab_pose` supply original clip axes and matched palm targets. Pose dictionaries carry flip/grab/manual/lip IDs, flip progress, lip balance and normalized crouch. Six flips/five grabs/two manuals/four lips are installed; nollie/switch modifiers remain excluded.

Ollie is hold-to-crouch/release-to-pop, base 8.8 m/s with up to 1.2 m/s charge over 350 ms. Crouch also pushes/pumps within existing caps. Fresh flips can repeat only after the previous 0.48 s clip; held grabs end on release. Family selection briefly consumes its direction instead of simultaneously spinning. Air stick and shoulders drive visible spin while physical flight velocity is preserved. Camera follows travel/facing and a world-up horizon, including reverse hysteresis and contact-normal body framing.

`world.get_transition_features()` snapshots three current-quay curve records from `TransitionPark.build`: id, world toe transform, radius, width, angle, exact StaticBody3D and coping points. Local surface is `(x,R*(1-cos(theta)),-R*sin(theta))`; +X is across. These are real front-face collisions with capped decks/sides and an accessible half-pipe bottom. Quay route Z67 remains protected; half toes use Z60.5.

`set_transition_features` installs them in Skater. `TransitionRide.try_start/step/reconcile` confirms exact live support, integrates signed tangent gravity/steer/explicit drive, handles finite sides/toe/lip and adopts actual collision-adjusted movement. Player capsule and visible contact frame follow the normal during short physical sweeps. A low-speed run rolls back; natural 90-degree departure preserves actual vertical momentum; only descending front support can reattach. Ordinary untagged walls do not enter the state. Stale floor flags cannot replace an active aerial exit with ground velocity.

Lip admission requires confirmed uphill contact within 6 degrees of the actual coping, lateral speed ≤2.5 m/s, a live held Grind request and clear actual movement/final upright capsule. The support ray is 0.5 degrees inside the final segment to avoid ambiguous shared deck edges. One catalogue entry/link is emitted. Left/right balance and four-second hold limit apply; release drops inward, released Ollie pops, bail/reset clears all. Main continues existing air/rail/manual/lip combos with its unchanged authoritative landing deadline and three-second overtime; supported transition travel alone is not continuation. Both manual variants and shuvits/lips classify correctly in existing side challenges.
