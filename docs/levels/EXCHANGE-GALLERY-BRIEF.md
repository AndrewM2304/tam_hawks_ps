## Inspected architectural evidence

The [reference dossier](../research/EXCHANGE-GALLERY-REFERENCES.md) records four actual historical photographs, HES LB32818 and the public GCHT conservation description. Lead design independently opened the four photos. GoMA's real front has eight Corinthian columns on a low continuous plinth, stacked round column-base mouldings, a recessed doorway plane and broad shallow stone steps. Warm cream/yellow ashlar, grey/dark rectangular paving and paler coping/step bands establish the material hierarchy. The 2017 close view shows people sitting on rounded/canted end blocks. The 2008 west-end image shows low curved pale edge bands and an open passage; its six-column bank frontage is a different building, not GoMA.

The new **eight-column entrance is an original compressed interpretation** of that observed gallery cue. The Y2.65 landing, 0.65m rise, banks, ledges, west Y0.45 platform, court stairs/fountain arrangement and all route coordinates below are authored gameplay dimensions. No source supplies measured heights or current café/shop tenancy for them. No photograph, banner, logo, map or artwork becomes a game asset. The west storefront remains a fictional composite.
