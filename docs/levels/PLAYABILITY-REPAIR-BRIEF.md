**17 October 2026. Active repair brief following the owner's actual playtest.** The owner reports a first-person/no-skater view, skating that does not feel convincing, and falling through the level. Both +20° and −20° slope coast runs lose speed from 15 to approximately 13.49m/s over two seconds. These are agent-supplied actual movement measurements, not an inspection of original THPS physics or proof of target hardware behavior. The response report separates measured old behavior, the new helper's analytical targets and pending integrated after measurements.

## First-minute and mistake-recovery review

Use the normal supported spawn near `(-22,0,28)` and its ordinary production camera. The west shopfront edge remains an optional early bank/low ledge; the fountain and gallery banks remain visible choices. The easy X−22 approach and open court are the baseline rather than a mandatory precision line.

1. Push north, release to coast, brake, then steer through broad left and right arcs on the open court. The rider stays visible and the camera settles without entering the torso or a facade. Turning should communicate a board travelling through space rather than an instantaneous world-axis change.
2. Ride the gentle west bank or gallery approach with ordinary input, then try an ollie with a small steer correction. Check a clean landing and a deliberate early/late miss. Both should leave a useful route back into the court. Do not require the previously proven exact 6.5m/s gallery-ledge acquisition to establish that basic skating works.
3. Approach a facade and a close corner, brake/turn away, reverse the steering choice and leave. Inspect the most retracted camera position, not only the open approach. Keep board, feet and landing visible while avoiding harsh snap/release behavior.
4. Explore each repaired north/south close, leave its centerline, cross every floor seam in both directions and return. Inspect the mouth from ordinary chase height so a solid boundary does not misleadingly look like a playable tunnel.
5. Repeat one optional grind/bank line, one missed river-transfer recovery and the existing safe route. Then retain the all-ten career circuit and Mo/Rae/Mairi regressions. A score success does not excuse a camera or floor defect encountered elsewhere.

Tests may use isolated fixtures to diagnose a camera solver or measure tuning, but the final integration evidence must also use the actual main scene and normal input actions.
