The contract supersedes historical Clyde coordinate locks in [BARRAS-CLYDE-BRIEF](BARRAS-CLYDE-BRIEF.md) where explicitly listed; stable career/save/gap/encounter identities survive.

The purpose is one legible river pocket containing two different repeatable skating circuits. It is not a new area, a uniform scale, another scenery pass or a claim of work on the Tony Hawk games. Keep the existing original Glasgow-inspired suspension portals, joined terrace silhouette, wide visible river and intact skating features. Remove surplus reachable ground before spending any art budget.

## Two different circuits to prove

These are intended physically plausible relationships using existing features. Neither is marked complete by this document. Separate local grind→air→manual lines with banked/steered returns establish local feature feasibility only; they cannot close the connected half-pipe→quarter or broken bridge→yard→safe crossing circuit gate. A successful whole return can contain a bank; any claim of an uninterrupted combo must show actual score continuity and real linking inputs.

**Vert/street circuit:** approach the relocated half-pipe through its open south end at Z65.5; air or lip on a real face; land/revert/manual, then leave through that open end. Use the Z69 quay lane around the quarter-pipe's south end, turn into its **east-facing approach `(−11,2,62)`**, then pop/air or lip there. Land toward the east and turn on supported quay back toward the pipe. The half-pipe centre and quarter toe are 27 m apart in X; that is not a claim of a 27 m drivable direct transfer through the quarter structure. The quarter-to-pipe return must remain a real ground route, with the next face visible before committing. A low quay coping/retained bench edge is an optional street variation, not a mandatory extra prop. No automatic spine transfer, forced turn, instantaneous yaw or deck-to-deck jump is assumed.

**River/yard circuit:** approach the translated optional deck at X20; use the existing broken-bridge rail or charged deck opening, land on the true south deck, then choose the yard hip or the 9.4 m ledge near X38…48. Release onto the supported south quay/yard, join the real water-facing embankment edge between the bridge mouths or manual along its safe side, and return through the continuous crossing at X−25. Its north arrival reveals the vert/street pocket and the other crossing. This is a different risk/height/direction sequence from the pipe circuit, not a second clockwise river lap. The native long-gap approach is optional: a beginner can use the continuous crossing in both directions, enter the yard without jumping and retry the broken bridge promptly after a miss.

The south return's 45 m crossing interval must earn its distance through existing usable bank-edge/manual choices and a readable next bridge. A curb count or long held balance is insufficient by itself. If actual play reveals a compulsory blank interval or blind approach, rework the relationship before accepting the slice; do not add scatter props to make a proximity metric green.
