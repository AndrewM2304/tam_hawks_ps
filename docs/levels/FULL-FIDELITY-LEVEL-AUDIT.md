24 October 2026. This supersedes the map scope/counts in the earlier [overnight audit](OVERNIGHT-SURFACE-AUDIT.md). ## What changed and why

The level now publishes **28 feature definitions**, **55 navigation routes** and **139 grind tracks**. Its post-build static inventory contains **436 collision bodies**, **56 eligible opaque wall bodies** and **84 bodies associated with grind tracks**, including the seven actual transition coping colliders. These counts include transition construction and rail-only collision; they are not independent counts of trick approaches. Sixty-four box-edge tracks derive from selected actual colliders; the remaining 75 tracks are authored paths. Dynamic actors, vehicles, activity sensors and the player's body are outside this level-only snapshot.

They occupy quiet space beside the existing career circuits, rather than obstructing those circuits. The undercroft deck stays below its real ceiling. The park additions follow the hill along each bench while keeping the narrow seat level across the slope; their lower side reads as retaining masonry instead of a twisted strip or an edge buried in grass.

## Density measured against playable routes

`world.get_level_density_audit()` samples the non-jump published navigation at at most six-metre intervals. At each sample it measures horizontal distance to an actual grind track or authored feature within 2.5 m vertical difference. Samples are route-weighted: overlapping routes produce repeated samples. This is a deliberately bounded opportunity/proximity measure, not pathfinding, an area coverage percentage or proof every direct approach is reachable. Native contact checks and actual player runs provide separate evidence.

## Surface affordances and honest exclusions

`world.get_surface_audit()` remains a defensive per-collider inventory with actual shape/layer information, stable semantic IDs, wall eligibility/reasons and grind track references. New banks/manual decks, return seats, graded park ledges and climbing handrails have finite published tracks and native contact evidence. Original rail IDs, ten career identities and the three original encounter identities remain unchanged.

Opaque box walls need at least 1.6 m height / 2 m face length; convex retaining geometry needs an actual exposed face of that scale beside real lower support. The new graded park retaining masonry contributes a qualifying wall body; the final count follows actual exposure after terrain grading. Thin suspended slabs are not counted as tall walls because the floor below is far away. Live native side probes and movement sweeps remain authoritative; metadata cannot manufacture a wall plane beyond the actual collision face.

Retained iron perimeter fences remain safety boundaries. Canvas/glass/water, thin props and overhead shelter are not opaque supporting walls. Stocked market counters retain their merchandise and hanging canopy valances rather than offering visually clipping standing grinds. Decorative benches/bins beyond boundaries and the vertical eight-metre scenic foundation skirts remain non-playable scenery; the skirts add no horizontal catch floor or collision. The park's shallow stone shoulder is roll-over terrain detail, not a raised grind ledge. Independent actual lap driving exposed the former narrow trim floating about 25 cm above lower grass at some crossings. Its replacement samples both sides of an 80 cm shoulder from real support; the mesh, collision and support queries share those exact triangles. High monument-cap tracks still require an advanced approach; positioned capture tests do not establish ordinary-ollie reachability.

The underlying six-district presentation remains a development build. New decks use existing cleared stone/paving materials, original steel edge details and restrained geometry. Transition surfaces use original muted procedural concrete grain with consistent world-space scale. Source-bound ground/chase inspection and human visual acceptance remain distinct from passing collision tests; final realistic art is not claimed.
