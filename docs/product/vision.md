# Product vision

**Working title:** Tam Jaws Pro Skater. **Product:** an original, single-player, arcade 3D skating game set in a compressed fictional Glasgow. The user's spelling “scater” is treated as the requested title's spelling variant, not a new mechanic. Final naming and trademark clearance belong to release preparation.

The target experience is a two-minute run that makes the player want another attempt: find a line, ollie a gap, join tricks, spot a shortcut, knock a cone off a fictional Wellington-inspired statue, and immediately restart. Free skate supports exploration and practice. Skating feel takes precedence over visual detail.

## Release boundary

- Godot 4.6.3 / GDScript; standalone macOS app, Apple Silicon primary, 1080p and 60 fps target.
- DualSense first with full keyboard fallback.
- One connected level: George Square, Royal Exchange Square, Central Station surroundings, and Barras/Barrowlands, with fictional connecting streets.
- Ollies, flips, grabs, spins, grinds, manuals and reverts; combos, multipliers, named gaps and special tricks.
- Ten career objectives including three score tiers; local records, goals and unlocks; secrets and environmental events.
- Original environment, ambience, skating SFX, ambient NPCs and short voiced reactions.
- Menus, readable HUD, results, pause, fast restart and accessibility settings.

Online multiplayer, simulation physics, open-world mission structures, character creation, complex dialogue and extra cities are excluded. Additional game engines, online accounts and online leaderboards are not implied.

## Delivery constraints

The current budget is zero additional spend. Local code, procedural art and synthetic local sound effects are the initial asset strategy. No paid voice API or purchased asset is permitted. Voiced NPC reactions remain in the release requirements, but are gated on an approved free, lawful, local voice source; silent caption prototypes must not be declared the final voice feature. A change to that scope requires the owner's decision.

The supplied plan ends after M03. M00–M03 mirror its stages. M04–M07 in the backlog are a proposed continuation assembled from the supplied full Release 1 scope, not recovered missing source text. Their order may be refined without silently deleting a release requirement.

## Acceptance authority

Automation can verify numbers, state transitions, resources and repeatability. The owner must play the controller milestone builds and judge responsiveness and enjoyment. A09 provides independent verification; A01 integrates and reports evidence. Neither role can certify real Apple Silicon hardware performance from this Linux development environment.
