# Glasgow encounters and playful side challenges

17 October 2026. These are original encounters for Tam Hawks Pro Skater, alongside the ten stable career goals. Current implementation contains the replayable busker and an ordered-coping mechanical-cat workshop; other ideas below remain explicit design tasks. No new career IDs, unlocks or save schema are introduced.

## Design aims

The best local joke should also create a skating problem: choose a line, judge speed, use a landmark and commit to a finish. Give the person a reason to ask, show the result in the world, and make retry immediate. A short caption explains the task; the skater's real movement/tricks provide the evidence. Funny dialogue alone does not complete an encounter.

The historical [character/performance research](../research/GLASGOW-CHARACTERS.md) supports street music, dancing and recognisable public places. The people, requests, props and dialogue here are fictional.

## Implemented: Mo's Big Finish

**Character/place:** Mo, an original guitarist at the east edge of the Exchange fountain court, `(28, 0, 31)`, facing into the court. The visible NPC reuses the cleared anatomical head/hands and original skinned garments; a newly authored acoustic guitar, strings/frets and open case establish the occupation. Gentle head/strum animation responds to success. Props have no collision on skating lines. A quiet original synthesized plucked open-chord texture plays through one spatial audio source, fading with distance and ending at 28 m. It contains no melody, licensed song or copied performance recording. Master and ambience sliders both control it; menus pause it and run expiry stops it. Human listening quality remains unverified.

**Request:** “Gie it laldy! Bank 1,000 with 3 trick types in this court. 35 seconds.” One clean line must contain three distinct trick families. A real ollie, flip and grab followed by a clean bank is one achievable route; a grind/manual/spin route offers replay variety.

**Trigger and rules:** Interact within 4.2 m horizontally and 2.5 m vertically. The player must first resolve any existing combo. During the 35-second active attempt, trick events and the resolved bank must remain within 34 m of Mo horizontally and 10 m vertically. Only positive real trick callbacks contribute. The entire qualifying combo must be observed during the attempt; leaving the court invalidates it until it resolves. Repeating the same family does not supply additional types. A short clean bank clears the candidate and leaves time for another line. A bail or timeout fails the attempt. Career expiry visibly ends an unfinished attempt.

**Feedback/replay:** The HUD retains timer, type count and the 1,000-point target independently of combo and transient dialogue. A world sign shows active progress or the result. Success says “Ya dancer!” and the guitarist plays an excited strum. Return to Mo and interact to replay after success/failure. Restart/mode changes clear the session-local attempt and result.

**Score/save behavior:** The encounter observes existing score callbacks and never manufactures an award, advances a career goal, grants an unlock or writes a profile. Existing career scoring remains authoritative: the skating bank can still earn the ordinary career score goals, while the side-encounter completion itself adds no goal or unlock. The challenge works in free skate and career, with the same pause and run-end gates.

**Runtime contract:** `game/characters/glasgow_encounters.gd` is a pausable `Node3D`. Main owns wiring. `configure([{id, position, heading}])` accepts `busker_big_finish`. `poll(position, interact, delta, combo_value)` runs only during active play. `on_trick(name, points, position)`, `on_bank(points, position)`, `on_bail()`, `finish_run()` and `reset_run()` receive actual gameplay transitions. `nearby_prompt(position)` returns action wording without a hardcoded key. `status_snapshot()` returns `id`, `title`, `state`, `remaining`, `family_count`, `required_families`, `target_points`, `attempts`, `wins`, `reason`, `detail`, `hud_text`, `position` and `combo_valid`. `feedback(text)` supplies transient captions; `reduced_motion` freezes decorative performance animation. `set_mix(master, ambience)` accepts raw gains, clamps each to 0–1 and controls the spatial instrument with their product. Native pausable processing controls playback during menus. `busker_sound.gd` caches one bounded eight-second, 22,050 Hz mono PCM loop across instances; the Dummy audio driver skips playback while retaining inspectable sample data.

**Evidence:** The focused construction/state smoke ran Godot 4.6.3 headless, built the actual NPC and exercised a successful bank with clean engine logs. An audio-data probe inspected the cached 352,800-byte, eight-second PCM: peak 0.219916 of full scale, zero clipped samples. This does not establish audible quality. The source-stable level capture subsequently performed real interact → ollie/flip/grab → clean landing/bank, asserting three families and exactly one win. [Offer](../testing/screenshots/level-redesign/11-busker-offer.png), [active line](../testing/screenshots/level-redesign/12-busker-active-line.png) and [success](../testing/screenshots/level-redesign/13-busker-success.png) show the actual 1,950-point result; the [medium view](../testing/screenshots/level-redesign/18-busker-medium.png) inspects the NPC. Independent gameplay tests are owned by QE in `tests/glasgow_encounters_tests.gd`; final aggregate results belong in the handoff. A stationary ollie/flip/grab currently qualifies, so rule completion does not prove line difficulty, discovery or fun.

## Implemented candidate: Cat's Got the Beat

**Character/place:** Rae, an original fictional tinkerer, and an original copper/teal clockwork cat occupy the raised George civic workshop terrace at `(0, 4.65, -98)`. The mechanical animal has a metal motor shell, folded ears, piston legs, steady brass eyes and a hinged tail on a turntable; it is a newly authored toy/sculpture, not the historical street performer's cat or routine. Rae uses the already cleared generic anatomy and original workwear/tool geometry. No new external asset, voice, recording or music is required. Existing skating/grind/bank audio supplies gameplay feedback.

**Request:** “Charge the wee cat! Grind A > B > C, then land and bank. 60 seconds.” The three independent six-metre coping tracks run east at Z=-91/Y=4.60: A from X=-16 to -10, B from -4 to 2, C from 8 to 14. Six-metre ground gaps provide room to land/relaunch and respect the existing recapture cooldown. A supported approach begins at `(-28, 4, -91)`; the finish at `(22, 4, -91)` has a permanent return via Z=-74. Numbered world plates and the HUD state the order; colour is supplementary.

**Rules:** Interact within 4.2 m horizontally and 2.5 m vertically, with no pending combo. A 60-second attempt uses the 32 m horizontal area around `(0, 4, -91)` and a ten-metre vertical bound. Only the player's real `grind_captured(rail_id)` event advances the exact IDs `Grind_Cat_A`, `Grind_Cat_B`, `Grind_Cat_C`. Proximity is insufficient. Duplicate callbacks for the latest marked rail are ignored; unmarked rails are ignored. A wrong marked rail resets the sequence to zero, except A restarts it at one. Captures may span separate clean combos, but success requires a positive resolved bank after the third capture, inside the area. Earlier, zero and negative bank events cannot finish the challenge. Bail, timeout, leaving the area, cancellation or career run expiry fail the whole attempt; retry resets all three indicators. Callback boundaries are checked even if no polling frame occurred first.

**Payoff/replay:** Each accepted coping steadily charges one labelled stage indicator. The final clean bank awards one win immediately and queues the payoff; the HUD/world sign tell the player to return to Rae to see it. The original 3.2-second paw-tap/head/tail movement starts only when the player is within ten metres horizontally, within 2.5 m vertically, and the stage point `(anchor + Vector3(0, 0.8, 0))` is inside the actual current camera frustum. This fixes the eastbound finish hiding the cat behind the player, without taking over the camera. It plays once and settles; the indicators remain charged. Reduced motion instead shows an immediate static raised-paw success pose with steady indicators and no flashing. Toggling reduced motion on during playback stops it without later replay; if it was enabled before any playback, turning it off can requeue the still-unseen one-shot. Return to Rae and interact to replay. Restart/mode change resets session-local attempts/results. Main arbitrates offers so accepting one side challenge cancels the other with clear feedback; no career IDs, unlocks or save schema are added.

**Runtime contract:** `game/characters/cat_encounter.gd` is a pausable Node3D. `configure([{id,position,heading,sequence,area_center,area_radius}])` accepts `cat_got_the_beat`; it rejects non-finite geometry or anything other than three distinct nonempty rail IDs. `poll(position,interact,delta,combo_value,audience_visible=false)`, `on_grind_capture(id,position)`, `on_bank(points,position)`, `on_bail()`, `cancel_attempt()`, `finish_run()` and `reset_run()` receive main's actual gameplay transitions. `nearby_prompt(position)` has no hardcoded input key. `status_snapshot()` supplies `sequence_index`, `next_rail_id`, `required_ids`, `state`, `remaining`, `attempts`, `wins`, `position`, `area_center`, `area_radius`, `reason`, `hud_text`, `celebration_state` (`queued`/`playing`/`finished`/`static`, or `none` outside success) and `celebration_started`. `feedback(text)` carries transient dialogue; `reduced_motion` immediately updates the prop. Actor `RaeWorkshop.start_celebration()` / `stop_celebration()` own the bounded one-shot; `animation_snapshot()` exposes its state for independent checking, without changing challenge progress. Restart/replay clears the queue; run expiry stops or cancels a pending/playing payoff while retaining the already earned win.

**Evidence:** Independent `tests/cat_encounter_tests.gd` passes **38/0**, with clean engine/script logs, including the queued-visibility correction. The real 1,050-point bank stays queued while the stage is outside the actual production camera frustum; a physical return starts the one-shot after 51 ticks at 9.87 m with the stage in view, then completes the full 3.2 seconds exactly once. Range/frustum gates, pause, stable reduced-motion pose/indicators, mid-playback reduced-motion stop, and no replay/extra reward pass. The actual mapped offer → A/B/C ollie transfers → clean 1,050-point bank succeeds exactly once with 52.32 seconds remaining and exactly the three attributed IDs. Order/duplicates/bank/area/deadline, production pause, first-tick career expiry, restart/results, an actual manual-balance bail, free-skate isolation and bounded/reduced-motion payoff checks pass. The [offer](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/27-cat-offer.png), [workshop medium](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/28-cat-workshop-medium.png), [A](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/29-cat-capture-a.png), [B](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/30-cat-capture-b.png), [C](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/31-cat-capture-c.png) and [success](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/32-cat-success.png) frames show actual production progress/banking without injected score or capture events. Only the medium hides the unrelated rider/HUD for asset framing. An isolated pose fixture additionally inspects the prop's movement; it is not win evidence. The original ordinary success faced east with the cat behind the skater; the implemented queued return/frustum gate addresses that concrete presentation finding. Independent actual-return/frustum checks now pass. The corrected-source [earned payoff](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/33-cat-earned-payoff.png) shows the real one-shot after the physical return, and [reduced motion](../testing/screenshots/court-cat/34-cat-reduced-motion.png) shows the public preference's static raised-paw salute. These two use an authored inspection camera after the actual production-frustum trigger; neither injects capture events, score, a win or animation time. All final actor/label/progress/payoff frames were inspected without a visible contact or clipping blocker for this alpha. No human fun, local tone, final-art or target-hardware acceptance is claimed.
