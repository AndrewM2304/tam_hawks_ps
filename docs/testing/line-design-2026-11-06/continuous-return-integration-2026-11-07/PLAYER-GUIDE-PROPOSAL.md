# Suggested optional Player Guide addition

Root owns the actual Player Guide write. This text is a player recipe, not a
one-combo or whole-map acceptance claim. Names below use default bindings;
existing remapping/controller caveats remain in the guide.

## East quay seats and wall return

Try the two long seats on the north side of the Clyde quay. Ollie onto the first
long edge with **Space/Cross** and **L/Triangle**, pop out and hold a **Manual
(M/L2)** across to the angled second seat. Balance with **W/S** and steer with
**A/D**. Catch its long edge, then pop past the end onto the paving.

Keep the Manual through a turn toward the landward masonry wall. Release the
Manual and push into the approach, then coast before your next Ollie. The
opening relay banks here: this is a connected route with two score segments.

Approach the wall at a glancing angle travelling west, jump while holding Grind,
then release another Ollie from the wall to Wallie back onto the quay. Catch a
Manual as you land and turn back toward the first seat's city-side edge.

After catching that edge westbound, ride briefly, then hold **Right/D** as you
pop to jump outward onto the north paving. Release Right once airborne and
catch a Manual on the clear landing. The wider riverside path remains available
as a simple travel bypass.
