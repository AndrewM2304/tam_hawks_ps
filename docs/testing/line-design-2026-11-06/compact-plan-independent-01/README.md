# Independent compact-default design review

**Later owner correction:** After this exact draft was reviewed, the owner said Warehouse was too small and requested a San-Francisco-like scale. The following findings remain the historical review of its exact preserved bytes; the medium dense urban replacement requires its own source/geometry/line review.

The Merchant Yard concept matches the owner's request to replace distant, sparse areas with a busy connected default level. **The exact draft needs a hall-to-basin topology revision before a graybox can establish its flow.** This is an offline design review, with 33 source/hash/arithmetic checks and no engine run. Those checks are not 33 gameplay assertions or acceptance of the proposed layout.

The reviewed draft is preserved as [JSON](proposal.json.gz) and [prose](proposal.md.gz), with exact hashes in [the independent report](independent-review.json). All 15 proposal source pins matched current files, and source and draft bytes remained unchanged during this review. The [portable audit source](independent-audit.py.txt) records the actual calculations; it intentionally uses repository-root paths and does not run Godot.

The main findings are:

- The hall-to-basin portal is centred at X34 on the declared solid warehouse edge. Its six-metre opening starts at X31, nineteen metres east of Hall's X12 edge. An eight-metre grade does not connect those regions. A real passage/cutout or revised portal/mass footprint is required.
- The mezzanine at Y2.2 has only two metres below the roof underside at Y4.2. Explicitly separate grounded sheltered travel from open upper trick space; standing room cannot establish full body/board/camera clearance.
- The courts cover 5,672 m² and listed solid masses 3,158 m² of the 9,856 m² outer rectangle. The remaining 1,026 m² may contain useful passages and returns, but its exact purpose and supported boundaries are still unspecified.
- The new basin opening must split the old continuous SouthRim grind guide as well as its real wall/display/support geometry. New broad floor geometry must preserve bowl and lower-street voids. Keep the separately loaded City Tour module intact.
- Six distinct returned lines and three closed cross-court single-combo circuits need exact feature positions and event graphs. Reference distance arithmetic, a route overlay or two separately banked lines cannot establish those outcomes.
- The proposed lazy world factory, optional activity guards and level-domain save migration are necessary. FeatureHub currently constructs and later calls all old activities; career validation accepts fixed geographic IDs. New goal data, complete optional-object guards and backward-compatible profile handling must accompany the new world.

The retained unit-scale facility is a sound starting point. Its source-defined east entry rotates to the proposed yard portal. Its three-metre secondary stair bypass should remain distinct from the draft's four-metre **main** bypass target; no scale change or nominal-width relabelling is warranted.

The owner's new direction supersedes the older requirement to retain six geographic arenas in the **default** map. That requirement was not passed. Preserve its source-bound registry, receipts and City Tour content. Native contacts, score deadlines, Music isolation, credible moving presentation, licensing and target-device evidence remain required. All native, rendered, cost, human and M5 results for the proposed compact level remain **Unmeasured**. The inspected Warehouse footage is design reference; its edits do not prove one continuous combo.
