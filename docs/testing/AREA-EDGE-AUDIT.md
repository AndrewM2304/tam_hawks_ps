## New semantic edge registration contract

`SkateEdges.build(world)` runs once after actual world/furniture construction and returns `GrindLine` objects. It adds no scene nodes, meshes, materials, collision, routes, scores or per-frame process. Box edges use their actual `CollisionShape3D.global_transform`; the visible court-seat cap and the street bench slat envelope have explicit bounded offsets. Source metadata is `art_label` for selected props and `geometry_id` for actual park border surfaces. Generated IDs begin `Grind_Edge_`, followed by family, quantized authored position and side/part. Existing coincident lines are retained under their old IDs.

Each straight edge stops a small distance before its corner (at most 0.12m), rather than promising a new automatic 90-degree rail-link mechanic. Candidate portions buried by adjacent authored terrain are cropped. New registrations remain above supported ground; the helper is not a blind scan of arbitrary window, leaf, roof or mesh triangles. Root's spatial lookup is required as total line coverage grows; adding metadata does not make capture-query cost free.

## Deliberate exclusions and remaining work

“Every reachable edge” is an acceptance target, not a completed claim from this first helper.

- All 58 perimeter fence families are not automatically registered. Their 3m tops normally exceed a flat jump, but a raised deck/prop can change accessibility. Each reachable section needs actual approach, collision/camera clearance, supported-side release and endpoint tests; an outside-void exit must be identified before calling it a safe line. Long fence curves would also enlarge every grind query unless spatially bounded.
- High roofs, gallery/civic cornices, portal trim, suspension chains, windows, foliage and merchandise are not treated as arbitrary automatic rails. Reachability/physical support must be established separately where the owner can actually land. Roof collision alone does not prove a useful approach/return.
- Flush road/terrain joins, tiny drain seams and buried wall tops are not visible skate ledges. Quay bridge entrances and upper platform ramp mouths remain ordinary floor.
- Existing decorative bollards, lamps, bins and art-only background facades need a separate reachable-prop collision review. A bench fix does not silently certify all street dressing.
- The outdoor finite terrain contour and explicit water hazard remain intentional. Their visual context is unfinished; the rejected backdrop trials do not become accepted merely because the new edge helper adds no render draws.
