## What was independently verified

All **77 original input paths and 77 retained raw execution snapshots** match
both before/after source maps. The actual executed renderer/project copies,
12 mesh source/import hashes, seven texture source/import hashes, 32 scene PNG
hashes/decoded pixels and 34 decoded imported-mip PNG hashes/pixels agree with
the raw receipts. Import/render logs contain no recorded errors. All **44 actual
native LOD samples** equal their expected submitted primitive counts and retain
the declared sweep through both hysteresis directions.

Every variant uses the same recorded camera position, target, FOV and native
seed for each of the eight views. Actual fixture subtraction is 14 primitives
per frame. Both fitted and reduced four-slot frames submit **8 objects/8 draws**;
the two atlas controls submit **6/6**. These are isolated fixture counts, not
live-map draw differences. The new near model submits **9,864 triangles**,
compared with 18,392 in the fitted reference. Its unchanged mid/far views submit
3,544/648 triangles and are **pixel-identical to the fitted reference**. Near
pixels differ, as expected from the deliberately changed chamfers and normals.
Diagnostic pixel differences are recorded without an automatic visual threshold.

Actual imported map image-data sums are **3,495,320 bytes for both fitted and
reduced**, 3,503,512 for the compressed atlas and 6,998,751 for lossless atlas
colour. The new variant binds the same four original maps and exact shared
StandardMaterial3D instances as the fitted reference. Paint/metal remain scalar,
glazing uses its original repeated map, and no accent atlas is sampled by these
four slots. These measured Image data sizes are not resident GPU memory. All
four variants coexist in this comparison, so its aggregate allocations do not
measure an isolated live replacement's memory or speed.

The separate independent geometry audit already verifies every exported
material/position/authored-UV triangle against the immutable reduced-geometry
reference, every part's actual triangle union and all 246 near-part envelopes.
Mid/far complete attributes and indices are unchanged from the fitted reference.
Those geometric results are supporting controls, not substitutes for these
actual native frames.

## Static image assessment

All 32 original scene outputs were inspected through two four-column comparison
sheets. Original full-resolution images were also inspected for the close view
of all four variants and fitted/reduced threshold, oblique and foreshortened
pairs. The actual decoded mip chains were inspected together in a separate
sheet. The sheets are inspection aids; original native PNGs remain untouched.

The reduced facade retains the reference's window/door recesses, glazing phase,
painted frame layout, metal handle and threshold contact. No new opening,
separated trim or visibly displaced entry is supported in the matched views.
The threshold remains at the independently measured 0.53 m height; this static
fixture is not a fresh live collision or route test.

Chamfer highlights remain readable at the close, threshold and oblique views.
The one-segment profile produces slightly sharper edge lines and different
small highlight shapes than the fitted rounded corners; complete silhouette or
normal equality is not claimed. The foreshortened view retains its thin frame
and ledge profiles. This fixture's dark, shadowed frontage and static samples do
not establish moving-view highlight quality or final city-art acceptance.

The compressed-atlas close image retains faint vertical painted-fascia bands.
The reduced four-slot fascia has the smoother scalar-material appearance of
the fitted reference and lossless-colour control, with ordinary lighting/normal
variation still visible. The decoded deep atlas colour mips visibly mix their
paint/glazing/metal regions in both atlas controls. The four-slot path retains a
separate glazing map and scalar paint/metal, so it removes that cross-class atlas
sampling mechanism rather than replacing it with another colour atlas. This is
a scoped static quality finding; it does not claim all texture or shading
artifacts throughout the game are fixed.
