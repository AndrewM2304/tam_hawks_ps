# Ramp camera baseline and next implementation targets

19 October2026. MAP-03 remains open. This is evidence from the existing controller/camera, not a new camera implementation or human-feel pass.

`ramp-captured` is a production Main/chase/HUD trial on native AppleM5/Godot4.6.3, balanced1280×800. One disclosed initial half-pipe fixture, then held physical W only:12 simulated seconds/720 physics samples, six alternating wall visits, five natural departures/reentries,120.74m, zero bails or discontinuities. All17 sampled images save and pass the inherited full-body/frustum/chest-obstruction checks after correcting the observer's expected image dimensions. The earlier `ramp-baseline` physical trace is valid only as physical evidence: its960×600 inherited image expectation skipped1280×800 saves, and its initial acceptance omitted the accumulated capture errors. It is not accepted as graphical evidence.

Root inspected actual approach, steep climb, return/crossing and opposite-wall frames. On steep support the figure is strongly foreshortened; the camera can swing across the pipe during reversal even though the complete body points remain in frame. Existing geometric visibility therefore does not establish readable or comfortable presentation. The broad high rear view also makes the rider smaller than the requested close follow reference. The original concrete/brick setting and current art are blockout, not final visual acceptance.

The current physics-sampled projection diagnostic ranges from about10 to169pixels between the nominal board/head points (median140px), with a large sampled relative-camera-bearing change. Those are contact-axis endpoints, not a measured rendered silhouette. Camera interpolation, mixed physics/render cadence and PNG readback stalls mean the derived angular rate is **not** a continuous frame-level camera-speed measurement and must not become a fabricated pass/fail threshold.

## Next source slice

1. Collect a bounded native **post-draw, no-PNG-readback** camera continuity baseline during the same natural input, with timestamps, camera transform/quaternion, rider/contact/travel state and projected body landmarks. Capture phase images in a separate repeated trial. Preserve the exact trajectory and refuse per-tick pose/velocity forcing.
2. Compare tighter trial framing within the approved plan ranges (arm4.5–5.2m, normal height2.2–2.6m, FOV62–66°). Preserve native sphere sweeps, full rider/board visibility, world-up horizon, near/far planes, reduced motion and sensitivity. Do not use camera distance to conceal sparse layout.
3. Inspect the current body-facing→camera-heading coupling at low-speed rollback and vertical aerial return. `_direction_sign` guards signed travel relative to current facing, but transition-facing can itself turn180°; test whether that defeats the intended low-speed camera stability. Separately inspect `_choose`/`_usable` fallback to an independently clear endpoint, which can preserve visibility while producing an abrupt view change. Prefer nearby feasible orbit continuity with bounded return over a distant-side snap; keep clipping refusal.
4. Address the near-collinear camera/contact-axis view on steep ramps, rather than treating a frustum pass as readability. Test a modest collision-aware side component/vertical framing strategy using the actual normal and travel state. Do not change gravity, momentum, rider heading, collider size or scored spins to hide a camera defect. Inspect existing compatible/native facilities before substantial infrastructure.
5. Test isolated ground turns, actual slope rollback, low/medium/high quarter/half-pipe climbs, same-face returns, charged/tap exits, deck/lip/side cases, purposeful air spins, wall/canopy/corner occlusion and reduced motion. Compare camera continuity/landmark visibility and actual sampled silhouettes; retain physical trajectory/score regressions. Run required full checks after final production source, then calibrated frozen matched render admission on that source. A tighter default and a scripted pass cannot close owner comfort/fun acceptance.

After this camera/ramp slice, survey/lock MAP-04's actual shared station/park port cross-sections and budgets before moving terrain.
