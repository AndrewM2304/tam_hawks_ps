# Merchant architecture private prototype —7November2026

Original Glasgow composite following two owner-approved generated design studies,
not an actual city survey or claim about current tenants. Runtime source remains
private; concept approval is not visual/native/performance acceptance.

The existing Glasgow/Exchange/Barras dossiers establish continuous sandstone
upper-storey rhythm, construction joins, broad shared cornices, recessed entrances,
practical varied market joinery/stock, contrasting brick halls and roof trusses.
The studies suggest compact streets with visible return destinations, sandstone
sash windows/slate chimney rooflines and brick industrial thresholds. Use these
cues in six LD-owned street-facing frontage spans. Fictional signs are deliberately
readable original glyph geometry: Close Records, Merchant Books, Cross Grocer,
East End Repairs, Goods In and Foundry Works. No source photograph/map/logo enters
production. Existing shared materials and CC0 photographic maps only.
