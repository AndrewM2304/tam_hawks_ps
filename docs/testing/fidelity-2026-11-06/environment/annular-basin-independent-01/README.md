# Independent annular-basin review — exterior finish rejected

The environment architect independently read the exact executed helper/observer
and personally inspected **all nine original A/B/A PNGs and all three startup
proofs** in the [author packet](../annular-basin-01/README.md). No engine or
production edit occurred. The independent verifier passes 127 integrity,
projection/pixel and unchanged-resource checks; it explicitly rejects exterior
shadow finish rather than turning those checks into promotion approval.

Wide and close native views show the fine radial water bands disappear. The
right exterior contact shadow acquires a narrow detached light crescent. The
independent pixel calculation reproduces 772 changed pixels, maximum 88 RGB8,
all lighter in (1010,355)–(1120,415). Ground below Y440 is byte-identical; that
selected unchanged region cannot cancel the failed near-contact region.

Decoded RGBA8 hashes/dimensions match all 12 exact originals. Independent camera
basis/projection reconstruction agrees for all 18 later world markers; their
coloured pixel counts/centroids and Canvas samples agree, with unchanged native
process/physics brackets. All 798 before/after source hashes agree. Native
collider, sun, water and all non-basin geometry/material/render-state records
agree across modes; the basin changes only its mesh resource in those records.

The helper retains the original 192 side/bottom triangles and removes the
64-triangle hidden top fan, adding a 128-triangle annulus: 256→320 total. Its
clockwise top winding, UP normal, +X tangent, X/Z metre UVs and W=−1 are
consistent. The component report records exact original positions/UVs/handedness
and indices, while native normal/tangent repacking differs by up to 0.00336°;
those packed vectors are **not byte-identical**. The original documented custom
render bounds are retained. The existing water remains 5 mm above the rim, with
measured boundary mismatch below 0.477 µm and no projected hidden-top overlap.

The source creates an open inner boundary with no inward-facing basin wall or
upward-facing interior floor. Light entering that opening can reach an outward
wall from its culled back side. This independently supports the proposed cause
of the contact-shadow gap, but is **not a completed causal native test**. A
credible inward wall (128 triangles) plus interior floor (64 triangles) reaches
512 total without changing the exterior silhouette. Use a real basin volume,
not a hidden top disk placed close to the water. A new controlled native shaded
cohort must restore contact before promotion; root's revised ceiling does not
waive that requirement. Interior wall/floor normals, tangent handedness, seam,
closedness and actual buffer allocation need their own proof.

Candidate/restored-B draws agree in three views, with +64 shadow primitives.
Original-A has a warm/culling transient; the whole bracket does not prove draw
invariance or cost acceptance. Both mesh resources are constructed before the
first baseline view, so swap-time buffer counters alone cannot measure the
incremental allocation of creating the new mesh. No frame-time, moving-water,
whole-game, final-realism or MacBook Air M5 result is claimed.
