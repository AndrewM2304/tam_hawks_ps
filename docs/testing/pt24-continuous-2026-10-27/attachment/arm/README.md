## Smallest proposal rejected; bounded next decision

An elbow-pole change with unchanged wrist cannot fix this case: the wrist and
anatomical hand are already on the wrong side of the solid. Moving that wrist
is a necessary additional change. A fixed-shoulder, fixed-length two-bone arm can
reach a retracted ungripped wrist geometrically, but endpoint reach alone does
not certify actual sleeve/hand clearance. In particular, maximizing elbow
clearance may aim the fingertips back through the wall. No untested arm target,
limb stretch, root-limit increase or hidden mesh is admitted here.

A next bounded ignored candidate could keep the shoulder and 0.30/0.29 m lengths,
retract only this ungripped wrist and choose the nearest allowed elbow-circle
point, then sample the original native Skin/hand geometry for every candidate.
Its current finite face authority, finite-edge/released-panel behavior,
transition continuity and query budget require proof before production work.
Real Grab/Boneless/Pogo/tow/handplant palms and any residual grip blend must retain
their existing contacts; all non-target bones, board/shoes, physical state and
score must remain unchanged. This is a separate proposed maintenance slice.
