## Precise refusal stage

The first heuristic uses a worst-direction radius of the complete base/full-curl
hand-box union. The original native OBJ and explicit normalized curl yield a
126.52 mm radial bound; the heuristic then requires a 142.52 mm wrist-plane gap.
The real Flip 6 wrist is 81.51 mm behind the face, requiring 224.03 mm correction;
the later catch wrist needs 241.23 mm. Both exceed the unchanged 200 mm prototype
cap before a candidate native Box query. The Flip 6 count of four calls is exactly
near-arm baseline Box + two current face rays + far-arm baseline Box. Earlier
poses with smaller wrist penetration do reach native candidate queries and are
also refused by the conservative envelopes.

This rejects the first search, not the existence of a reachable real pose.
A separate read-only endpoint calculation using the fixed 0.30/0.29 m circle,
actual captured transforms, original hand-box union and existing free-hand basis
finds a Flip 6 candidate at 180 mm wrist retraction and approximately 38 degrees
of elbow-circle change: wrist 98.5 mm clear, elbow 95.9 mm clear, full hand box
58.0 mm clear. This is only offline feasibility, not native garment validation,
full timeline/corner coverage, a production implementation or acceptance.

A smaller sound search can avoid the unnecessarily isotropic hand precondition,
keep the elbow between its sleeve-clearance plane and the wrist plane, then let
the original oriented cached hand/sleeve envelopes and fresh native geometry
accept/refuse each candidate. If needed, the existing constant-weight rows also
admit conservative radial-cylinder support transformed by their exact affine
Skin map, avoiding empty rectangle corners without shrinking the actual mesh.
Any such refinement needs separately retained source and native results; this
rejection remains immutable. No refinement or further engine run is made here.
