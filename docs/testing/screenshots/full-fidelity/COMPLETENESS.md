The acceptance source is the owner's request for polished trick sets, connected
combinations, classic arcade skating functionality, useful walls and ledges, and
a thorough level review. A prior ADR or passing checkpoint does not reduce it.
The original product plan also requires a dense connected city, immediate replay,
meaningful exploration, two-minute goals, readable feedback and controller-first
play. All results below remain pending final source-bound integration and motion
review until an actual receipt is recorded.
