The architect inspected the original 1280×800 Compatibility and Forward+
`exchange-shopfront.png` and Compatibility `ordinary-spawn.png` under

Observed defects: large dark plates surround shallow upper panes; a repeated white
cross dominates every window; uniform chunky rectangular shop jambs have no joinery
profile; doors lack rails, stiles, bottom panels and real thresholds; records/books
are identical upright blocks; cafe and bakery merchandise are barely identifiable.
Normal spawn exposes repeated building edges and broad uninterrupted surfaces.

The proposed work is original architectural-composite design, not surveyed Glasgow
reconstruction. Existing original shop names and material resources remain.

1. Prototype true upper-window apertures in an original one-surface wall visual,
   with finite 180–220 mm reveals, recessed glazing, sash thickness, smaller meeting
   rails and projecting sloped sills. The complete original native wall collider
   remains. Preserve background/horizon extension and later-mutated arcade walls;
   those require a separate coordinated solution before shell replacement.
2. Profile shop pilasters, fascia, glazing beads and plinths using existing shared
   box batches. Recess doors behind the outer frame and construct their bottom
   panels/rails/stiles. Keep threshold coordinates and west raised-door correction.
3. Distinguish cafe, record, book and bakery displays by recognisable original
   silhouettes, uneven placement and construction rather than more random cubes.
4. Profile gallery window/door joins and the portico entablature while retaining
   all column/footing/roof datums and skating envelopes.

Pre-addition provisional wholeworld ceiling: **zero new texture bytes, zero new
shader/material resources, no collision/layout changes; at most +80,000 submitted
source triangles, +16 final static batch nodes across the entire world (never
16 per cell), +16 draw calls in a matched representative view, and +4 MiB native
geometry and instance buffers**. These are declarations, not measured results or admitted cost.
Replacing existing primitive pieces counts against the original, not as an excuse
to inflate detail. Native inventory must record exact baseline/candidate totals and
the number of optional occluder candidates affected by visible BoxMesh replacement.

with a finite opaque pane, use world-scale UVs and preserve native batch CPU
registration/material identity. The disabled fitted FacadeBay pilot keeps its
existing separate path; this work must not reopen or relabel its rejected costs.
